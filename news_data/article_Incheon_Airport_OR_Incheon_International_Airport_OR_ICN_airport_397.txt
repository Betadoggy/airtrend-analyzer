Title: Mercure Ambassador Seoul Magok Opens in Seoul’s Vibrant Gangseo District
Source: Hospitality Net
URL: https://www.hospitalitynet.org/announcement/41011952.html
--------------------------------------------------

Mercure Ambassador Seoul Magok offers a seamless blend of modern comfort and local charm for both leisure and business travellers. Its prime location places travellers near cultural attractions and entertainment venues, while its proximity to convention centres, MICE facilities, and corporate offices makes it an excellent choice for business visitors.

The hotel's design draws inspiration from the dynamic and innovative spirit of Magok District, seamlessly incorporating subtle elements of Korean design, local motifs, and environmentally friendly materials. With 400 guestrooms and suites, the hotel offers versatile accommodation options - Junior and Executive Suites feature separate living areas, ideal for extended stays and business travellers, while Deluxe Triple Rooms provide convenience for families or groups. Select rooms offer stunning views of Seoul Botanic Park and the rooftop gardens, creating a serene retreat within the city.

The hotel offers two dining experiences that celebrate local flavours and international cuisine. Moasis, the hotel's all-day dining restaurant, serves seasonal dishes in a vibrant setting with floor-to-ceiling windows. The stylish M Lounge & Bar offers a curated selection of wines, expertly crafted cocktails, and light bites, providing an inviting space to relax.

For wellness and relaxation, guests can enjoy the hotel's state-of-the-art fitness centre or unwind with a refreshing swim in the naturally lit indoor pool for a rejuvenating experience.

Conveniently connected to Seoul's transport network, the hotel is a 10-minute drive from Gimpo International Airport and a 40-minute drive from Incheon International Airport. Magoknaru Station on Subway Line 9 is a five-minute walk away, offering easy access to Seoul's renowned districts, including Gangnam, Myeongdong, and Hongdae.

Garth Simmons, Chief Operating Officer of Accor's Premium, Midscale, and Economy Division in Asia, commented, "The opening of Mercure Ambassador Seoul Magok marks a significant milestone in strengthening our presence in Seoul, one of Asia's most dynamic and high-potential cities. This hotel is a remarkable addition to the vibrant Gangseo district, strategically located to serve both leisure travellers and business communities. With Mercure reaching 1,000 hotels globally this year, we are excited to continue our upward momentum in Asia. This hotel will not only enhance our offering in Seoul but also create new opportunities for the neighbourhood, further supporting its dynamic development."

Yoon-Jung Lee, General Manager of Mercure Ambassador Seoul Magok, expressed excitement about the new opening, "We are excited to open the doors of Mercure Ambassador Seoul Magok and look forward to welcoming guests from near and far. Our goal is to provide a memorable experience that captures the spirit of this vibrant district, offering a comfortable and inviting space for both leisure and business travellers."

To celebrate its opening, Mercure Ambassador Seoul Magok is offering an exclusive opening package which includes complimentary breakfast for two adults and late checkout. The package, available from 27 November 2024 to 28 February 2025, starts at 205,000 KRW (excluding tax).
