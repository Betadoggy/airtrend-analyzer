Title: Priority Pass Members Can Now Use Allowance at Selected Restaurants in Singapore Changi Airport (SIN)

Miles are great for First Class and Business Class flights but if you are stuck in Economy and do not have lounge access, Priority Pass is a great way to gain access to some airport lounges which can be a real lifesaver when it comes to busy airports. There are plenty of credit cards that you can apply for to gain access to an airport lounge but my favourite ones are The American Express Platinum Card as well as the Citi Prestige Card - both cards may come with a non-waivable annual fee but it gives you unlimited access (+1 guest) to Priority Pass lounges around the world.

For those of you who have been 'in the game' for awhile now, you might remember The Green Market (TGM) in Terminal 2 - that was one of my favourite places to utilise my Priority Pass membership since it offers a decent meal before the flight. At some point, travellers also opted for the takeaway option as TGM was pretty flexible but unfortunately, this is currently not a participating location under the Priority Pass program. With that being said, the lounge network has added a handful of restaurants in Singapore Changi Airport (SIN) where members can utilise their benefits for a free meal.

At the time of writing, Priority Pass has added four restaurants to the program where members can utilise their lounge entitlement for a free meal:

Crystal Jade La Mian Xiao Long Bao (Terminal 1 Transit)

Kaveri Indian Vegetarian (Terminal 2 Transit)

Chutney Mary Indian Fast Food (Terminal 2 Public)

Crystal Jade Go (Terminal 4 Transit)

With the exception of Chutney Mary Indian Fast Food, all other restaurants are located within the transit area of the airport. Similar to actual airport lounge visits, you will need to present your boarding pass and your Priority Pass membership (digital card is fine) in order to redeem a free meal. Do note that the Priority Pass membership from The American Express Platinum Card will not be eligible for these 'Eat offers' but you will be able to utilise your membership from the Citi Prestige Card (+1 guest) for these.
