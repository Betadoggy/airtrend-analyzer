Title: BTS's V, BLACKPINK's Jennie, And EXO's Kai Trigger Huge Reactions With Latest Activity Together
Source: Koreaboo.com

BTS’s V, BLACKPINK’s Jennie, and EXO’s Kai are once again at the center of massive online attention thanks to their unexpected timing at the airport.

The three idols were spotted departing for Paris Fashion Week on the same day, leading fans to notice that they likely had even gotten on the same flight.

black

All three stars were photographed at Incheon International Airport within a short time frame, each heading to France to represent their respective brands at Paris Fashion week.

Jennie kept it chic and understated in a long navy coat layered over a beige top and pinstriped trousers, carrying a small Chanel bag.

V opted for a relaxed yet polished look with a checkered shirt, loose jeans, and a loosely tied black necktie, completing it with layered necklaces.

Kai went for a cozy and classic airport style, wearing a camel-colored sweater over a white shirt paired with light blue jeans.

Given the past dating rumors between Jennie and V, as well as Jennie’s confirmed relationship with Kai in 2019, many couldn’t help but joke about how awkward the flight might be. Others pointed out that it’s common for top stars to travel together during major international events like Fashion Week, though the coincidence still sparked lighthearted comments on a viral post on TheQoo.
