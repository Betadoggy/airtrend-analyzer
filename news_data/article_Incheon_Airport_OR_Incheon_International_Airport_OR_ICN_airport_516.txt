Coldplay concert in Hong Kong

Coldplay Hong Kong concert dates: April 9, 11, and 12 of 2025.

Ticket availability: Ticket sale starts on October 10, 2024. The price of tickets is yet to be announced.

Visa requirements: According to the Hong Kong government website as of October 1, 2024 (https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/services/visas/pre-arrival_registration_for_indian_nationals.html), Indian nationals must apply and successfully complete Pre-Arrival Registration (PAR) online before visiting Hong Kong.

An Indian national can apply for PAR by satisfying these two conditions:

Holds an Indian passport valid for at least six months; and

Intends to visit or transit (except in direct transit by air and not leaving the airport transit area) the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for a stay not exceeding 14 days.

PAR registration is free of cost. According to the Hong Kong Tourism Board, PAR must be applied 4 to 6 weeks in advance from the date of travel. If the PAR application is rejected, a visa will be required to travel to Hong Kong. As per travel experts, PAR must be applied before booking plane tickets. Generally, a PAR application is processed within a day or two.

"Each destination has its own criteria. However, a strong travel record always helps. However, places like the UAE, Azerbaijan, and Hong Kong (with its quick Pre-Arrival Registration) welcome travellers even without prior foreign travel experience," said Mohak Nahta, Founder & CEO of Atlys, a visa application service provider.

Coldplay concert in Seoul, South Korea

Coldplay Seoul concert dates: April 16, 18, 19, 22, 24, and 25, 2025.

Ticket availability: Tickets for the concert are available as of October 4, 2024. The ticket price ranges between Korean Won ?66,000 - 1,080,000 (about Rs 4,187 to 68,521). The currency was converted from Korean Won to Indian rupees on October 1, 2024, at 3 PM.

Visa: Nahta of Atlys says, "For Seoul, you need to take a sticker visa, which may, and it may take 25 to 30 days to get issued. The cost of this visa is about Rs 8,000."

Manoj Dharmani, CEO, DUDigital Global explains the documents needed for South Korean Visa:

Original current and old passport (if available). The current passport should have more than 6 months' validity.

2 recent passport photographs in size 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm, colour with a white background.

Copy of ITR of the last 3 years. (If ITR not available then, a letter of explanation mentioning the reason must be submitted).

Salaried have to submit a salary slips of the last one year (with a stamp and signature by HR of your company). A no objection certificate (NOC) letter from the company with a stamp and signature by the HR.

Original bank statements of the last 6 months with the bank's stamp with signature by bank authority (A4 size paper only),

Proof of return air tickets

Proof of hotel booking in a South Korean city.

Flight ticket: According to Skyscanner.com, flight tickets can cost between Rs 31,000 to Rs 41,000 (inclusive of tax) to travel from India to Incheon International Airport in Seoul, South Korea.

Source: Skyscanner.com as of October 1, 2024

Local Travel: You can take the premium AREX Incheon Airport Express Train to Seoul from Incheon International Airport. You can book this train and look up its schedule on its official website (https://www.airportrailroad.com/rt/tktBstk.do). The tickets are priced at KRW 9500 or about Rs 571 as of October 1, 2024 2pm. The express train can be taken to reach the Seoul city.

You can also book an 'International Taxi' (https://www.intltaxi.co.kr/?lang=en), which is driven by drivers who have specialized training and can speak English.

Hotel: According to Booking.com (as of October 1, 2024), the cheapest hotel near Goyang stadium (where Coldplay concert will happen) costs Rs 2,797 including taxes (Halla Livingtel) per night. This hotel is 1 km away from the stadium. Three-star hotels prices start from Rs 10,000 per night. The prices are converted to INR by Booking.com and shown to users.

Cost of SIM card: According to Matrix website (as of October 3, 2024), the 8GB data for 10 days plan costs Rs 2,948. This pre-activated SIM will pick up signals from KT Korea and is 3G/4G/LTE compatible.

Source: Matrix website as of October 3, 2024

Local sightseeing: According to the South Korean Government, "Seoul has an extensive network of public transportation and taxis. Trains run non-stop from approximately 5:30 am until midnight. All subway lines are colour coded and stations display signs in Korean, English, and Chinese."

You can also use the various bus services to travel around the city. (https://topis.seoul.go.kr/eng/page/transInfo_1_1.jsp)

"The TMoney Mpass is a transit card specifically designed for international visitors that require the use of public transportation multiple times a day. The card can be used up to 20 times a day for most subways and public buses. The TMoney Mpass can be purchased at the Seoul Station Tmoney Town. The Mpass is good for one month from the date of purchase, and will only be activated upon use," said the South Korean government on the visit Seoul website.

Source: Klook website as of October 4, 2024.

Coldplay concert in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

Coldplay Abu Dhabi concert dates: January 9, 11, 12 and 14, 2025, as of October 3, 2024.

Tickets availability: The official Coldplay ticket website still shows the availability of tickets. The concert tickets costbetween United Arab Emirates Dirham (AED) 195 (about Rs 4,449) and AED 1495 (about Rs 34, 176).

Visa: According to Nahta of Atlys, "Visa for Abu Dhabi is e-visa type and takes about 36 hours to be issued."

Dharmani of DUDigital Global says, "The Visa can cost in the range of Rs 8000 to Rs 11,000 depending on number of days for which visa is issued."

Flight ticket: According to Skyscanner.com, direct return flight tickets from India to Abu Dhabi (Zayed International Airport) ranges between Rs 17,742 and Rs 40,307. For example, the Delhi to Abu Dhabi return flight tickets is around Rs 26,800.

Source: Skyscanner.com as of October 4, 2024

Local Travel: According to Abu Dhabi Emirate's website (https://admobility.gov.ae/en/taxi-fares), there are three categories of licensed local taxis- silver, airport taxi, and Vito airport taxi. The silver taxi is the cheapest having a minimum fare of AED 12 (about Rs 273) and AED 1.82 (Rs 41.53) for every km.

"We license and organize the transport service using public and private taxi vehicles and license booking and dispatching service providers in order to meet the needs of the public in the Abu Dhabi Emirate," said the Abu Dhabi government on its website.

If you don't want to take a taxi, you can try the Abu Dhabi public bus service, the schedule of which can be found here-https://admobility.gov.ae/en/pb-bus-service/hafilat-public-buses-fees. However, you need an NOL card to travel by bus as cash payments are accepted. The bus fare starts from AED 5 (about Rs 114 as of October 3, 2024,).

Hotel: According to Booking.com (as of October 1, 2024), the lowest-price hotel costs around Rs 658 (Paradiso Backpackers Nest) inclusive of tax per night. This is 25 km from downtown Abu Dhabi. If you want to stay near downtown Abu Dhabi, the lowest priced hotel (MAMA DELIGHT GUEST ROOM) costs Rs 1,395 per night, including tax-.

Cost of SIM card: According to the Matrix website (as of October 1, 2024), a SIM card for UAE costs Rs 599 minimum. It offers 1GB per day for 7 days. This SIM card will pick up signal from DU, a UAE-based telecommunications company.

Source: Matrix website as of October 4, 2024

Local sightseeing cost: According to the travel website Klook.com, local sightseeing tours cost between Rs 608 to 10,143 (as of October 1, 2024). The Abu Dhabi Hop on Hop off bus tour costs Rs 6,774 and the Qasr Al Watan Presidential Palace Ticket in Abu Dhabi costs Rs 1,487.

Source: Klook website as of October 4, 2024

Food and beverage cost: According to the Trip Advisor website (as of October 1, 2024), dinner and lunch at reputable restaurants in Abu Dhabi can cost between Rs 684.00 and Rs 3,422.00.

All prices and other travel information for the Abu Dhabi trip are between October 1 and October 4, 2024.
