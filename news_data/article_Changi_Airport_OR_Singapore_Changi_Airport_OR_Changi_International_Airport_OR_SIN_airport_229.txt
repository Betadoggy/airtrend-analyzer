The Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card was relaunched on 19 May 2023 to replace the X Card with a completely new set of benefits and acquisition offers. From now until 30 September 2023, apply for the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card as an eligible new-to-card customer to receive up to 45,000 bonus miles (inclusive of 10,000 bonus miles with the payment of annual fee) with just a minimum spend of S$3,000 within 60 days of card approval.

Click HERE to apply for the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card!

The Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card can be categorised as a general spend credit card with an uncapped earn rate of 1.2/2.0 miles per dollar on local- and foreign-currency spend respectively, but it also offers the opportunity to earn 3.0 miles per dollar (capped at S$1,000 per statement period) on eligible online transactions made on Transportation, Food Delivery, as well as Groceries and Food Stores. In addition to the 2 miles per dollar earn rate on foreign currency spending, cardholders can also enjoy zero foreign currency fees in the months of November 2023 and December 2023, making it a great card to consider if you have travel planned for those months.

Get up to 45,000 Bonus Miles

Eligible new customers can receive up to 45,000 bonus miles with the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card and this is a fantastic offer that I highly recommend that you take advantage of:

10,000 Bonus Miles from payment of Annual Fee (S$194.40) - You will not receive this if you opt for an annual fee waiver in the first year.

35,000 Bonus Miles from spending a minimum of S$3,000 within 60 days of card approval - this is essentially 11.7 additional miles per dollar on the first S$3,000 spent which is incredible! As a point of reference, you will only need 30,500 KrisFlyer miles to redeem a First Class Saver ticket (which gives you access to The Private Room at Singapore Changi Airport) from Singapore to Jakarta. If you wish to go somewhere a little bit further, a Saver redemption in Business Class from Singapore to Hong Kong, Taipei, and cities in South China will only require 34,000 KrisFlyer miles.

In order to be considered as an eligible new customer, you must not currently hold any Standard Chartered credit cards as a principal cardholder (even though holding a supplementary otherwise is fine) and you must not have cancelled one in the last 12 months from the date that the Bank receives your application for the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card.

Earn up to 3 Miles per Dollar

The Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card offers 3 miles per dollar (capped at S$1,000 spent per statement period) on eligible online transactions made on Transportation, Food Delivery, as well as Groceries and Food Stores - these transactions are identified based on their merchant category codes. For all other eligible spending, the card offers an uncapped earn rate of 1.2 miles per dollar on transactions charged in SGD and 2.0 miles per dollar on non-SGD transactions.

Eligible Online Transactions: 7.5 Rewards Points per S$1 (3 Miles per Dollar)

Eligible SGD Spend: 3 Rewards Points per S$1 (1.2 Miles per Dollar)

Eligible Non-SGD Spend: 5 Rewards Points per S$1 (2 Miles per Dollar)

Zero Foreign Currency Fee in November and December 2023

As part of the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card S$0 Foreign Transaction Fees Promotion (01 November 2023 to 31 December 2023), cardholders can enjoy zero foreign currency fees on all eligible transactions charged in a foreign currency other than SGD for two full months. As part of the promotion, cardholders will be entitled to a 3.5% cashback (with no cap) on all eligible foreign currency transactions made during the aforementioned period - they will also continue to earn 2 miles per dollar on these transactions. The 3.5% cashback awarded under the promotion for eligible foreign currency transactions made and posted between 01 November 2023 and 31 December 2023 will be credited by 29 February 2024.

Rewards Points Do Not Expire

Eligible expenses charged to the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card will earn 360° Rewards Points and instead of having to keep track of when your points expire, it is worth noting that Rewards Points earned on this card do not expire. Rewards Points can be converted into KrisFlyer miles (25,000 Rewards Points : 10,000 KrisFlyer miles) via the 360° Rewards Redemption Platform, or one of the following airline and hotel loyalty programs via the SC EasyRewards Programme:

Emirates Skywards (Emirates)

Etihad Guest (Etihad Airways)

Flying Blue (Air France/KLM)

Frequent Flyer (Qantas)

Infinity MileageLands (EVA Air)

MileagePlus (United Airlines)

Privilege Club (Qatar Airways)

Accor Live Limitless (ALL)

IHG One Rewards

A transfer fee of S$27 (inclusive of GST) is charged for each conversion of 360° Rewards Points to airline or hotel loyalty points.

Travel Benefits

In addition to earning miles on the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card, the card also comes with additional travel benefits that cardholders can enjoy each year. Firstly, cardholders can enjoy two lounge visits with each membership year via Priority Pass - there are plenty of participating lounges around the world to choose from and you may even opt for non-lounge experiences at Singapore Changi Airport if you prefer. Secondly, by simply charging the full travel conveyance fare to the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card, your trip is covered by a travel insurance policy (with a coverage of up to S$500,000) underwritten by Allianz Insurance Singapore Pte. Ltd. (AIS). Lastly, you will also get to enjoy exclusive Journey Card promotions which may change from time to time.

Conclusion

The new customer sign-up bonus for the Standard Chartered Journey Credit Card is hard to pass up - getting 35,000 bonus miles with a minimum of S$3,000 spent within 60 days is a fantastic deal since you can also earn up to 3 miles per dollar on eligible online transactions (capped at S$1,000 per statement period) on top of that. While there are other credit cards in the market that offer more than 3 miles per dollar on eligible online transactions, these cards also come with their own set of intricacies and spending limits.
