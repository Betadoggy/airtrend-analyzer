Title: Work delay hits garden pavilion at Bengaluru airport’s T2
Source: The Times of India
URL: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bengaluru/bengaluru-airports-t2-garden-pavilion-faces-construction-delays/articleshow/113827366.cms
--------------------------------------------------

Bengaluru: The much-anticipated garden pavilion at Terminal 2 of Kempegowda International Airport (KIA), which was to offer international passengers a scenic walking experience, has run into significant delays. Initially expected to be completed within a year, the project has been under construction for two years, with no clear timeline for completion.

The 40-metre-high pavilion, designed to mirror Singapore's Changi Airport, was envisioned as a key attraction of T2 , offering passengers a 360-degree panoramic view of the airport. The pavilion was to serve as a vantage point where visitors could watch planes take off and land, with a nearby 20-metre monumental sculpture complementing the experience.

In addition to these features, the pavilion will host themed restaurants and art installations, which have contributed to the extended construction timeline. Sources in Bangalore International Airport Limited said the inverted bamboo basket structure, a central element of the pavilion, has already been completed. Six lakh plants from diverse ecological habitats have been raised.

However, the delay in completing the restaurant section has been a major factor in the project's slowdown. The pavilion, once completed, will offer a variety of amenities, including a treetop outdoor lounge, exhibition centres, and cutting-edge watering technology managed via a mobile application. "We are uncertain of the exact completion date, but we hope the work wraps up soon, and the pavilion becomes accessible to passengers. The restaurant work is the only remaining task," a BIAL spokesperson told TOI.
