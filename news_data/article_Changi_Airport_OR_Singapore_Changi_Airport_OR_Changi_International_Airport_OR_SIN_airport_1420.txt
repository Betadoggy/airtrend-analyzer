Title: ST Engineering posts record $18.7 billion in contract wins for FY2025
Source: The Straits Times
URL: https://www.straitstimes.com/business/companies-markets/st-engineering-posts-record-18-7-billion-in-contract-wins-for-fy2025
--------------------------------------------------

SINGAPORE – ST Engineering’s total contract awards for 2025 reached a record $18.7 billion, exceeding the $12.6 billion total in 2024 by 49 per cent, the group announced on Jan 28.

The full-year tally was lifted by about $4.7 billion in new contracts secured during the fourth quarter.

The contract wins were distributed across the group’s three main business clusters.

The defence and public security segment accounted for the largest share of the quarterly wins at $2.5 billion.

The commercial aerospace segment secured $1.7 billion in new contracts.

The urban solutions and satcom segment recorded about $500 million in contracts.

ST Engineering said these contracts are not expected to have a material impact on the group’s consolidated net tangible assets or earnings per share for the current financial year.
