Title: Samsung Galaxy Watch6 series sizes leak
Source: GSMArena.com
URL: https://www.gsmarena.com/samsung_galaxy_watch6_series_sizes_leak-news-59240.php
--------------------------------------------------

The Samsung Galaxy Watch6 series is coming on July 27 and it will consist of two models in two different sizes (themselves in two different connectivity packages). There will be two different models - a Galaxy Watch6 and the model that will bring back the rotating bezel - the Galaxy Watch6 Classic. The Watch6 will come in 40mm and 44mm sizes, while the Watch4 Classic will come in 43mm and 47mm.

The two watch models will also be available in Wi-Fi only and Wi-Fi + LTE. Samsung will offer pre-order perks in select markets.

Meanwhile, popular Korean football player Son Heung-min was spotted rocking a Galaxy Watch6 at Incheon International Airport. The star will be a Galaxy brand ambassador for Samsung.

Specs-wise, the Galaxy Watch6 models will use an Exynos W930 chipset with 2GB of RAM, and will pack 16GB of storage. The 40mm Watch6 and 43mm Watch6 Classic will have a 300mAh battery, while the bigger models will get a 425mAh.

Finally, the full press renders of the watches have surfaced (source link 3) and they appear to sport an identical look to the Galaxy Watch4 series - both vanilla and classic (the one with the rotating bezel).
