Title: Blackpink Singer Rosé Rewrites History And Beats Her Own Bandmate Jennie’s Record
Source: Forbes
URL: https://www.forbes.com/sites/hughmcintyre/2024/12/08/blackpink-singer-ros-rewrites-history-and-beats-her-own-bandmate-jennies-record/
--------------------------------------------------

The latest era of Rosé’s solo career is going pretty spectacularly, especially on the American music charts. The Blackpink singer introduced this second promotional period with the single “Apt.,” which is still on the rise on a number of tallies in the U.S. As the tune climbs and becomes a true radio smash, Rosé makes history and even manages to beat an all-time record set by another member of the same girl group.

“Apt.” jumps from No. 18 to No. 14 on this week’s Pop Airplay chart. The tune now stands as the highest-rising hit of all time by a solo female musician who comes from the K-pop genre.

Rosé isn’t originally from South Korea, though she became a star in that country. She was born in New Zealand, and she holds dual citizenship in the two nations. The Blackpink talent is still known primarily for her work in K-pop—both on her own and with the band—though she’s expanded her horizons with her latest offerings.

Last week, Rosé, joined by her collaborator Bruno Mars, lifted “Apt.” to a new peak of No. 18. As the single hit that mark, she matched the all-time record for the top position reached by any solo female K-pop musician. That ceiling was established only months before “Apt.” arrived, and by another Blackpink singer.

Jennie joined The Weeknd and Lily Rose Depp on the tune “One of the Girls,” which was released around the run of The Idol, the TV series that starred all three musicians. That tune reset the standard for the loftiest space any solo female K-pop artist had reached, but that record didn’t belong to Jennie for very long.

Rosé now claims the third-biggest hit ever by any solo K-pop artist on the Pop Airplay chart, and she and Mars may soon be able to lift again. “Apt.” comes in behind only Psy’s “Gangnam Style” (No. 10) and BTS singer Jung Kook’s “Left and Right,” a collaboration with Charlie Puth (No. 11).

“Apt.” is on the rise on the two Billboard-published radio rankings it appears on this week, as the single is turning out to be not just a sales and streaming win, but a major player on the American airwaves—something that’s still a relative rarity among K-pop acts. As it makes history on the Pop Airplay list, the same cut rocks its way to a new high of No. 31 on the Adult Pop Airplay chart.

Rosé is even on the rise on the all-style, all-language Radio Songs tally. The Blackpink vocalist sends her high-profile duet to No. 29, which is a new high for the tune—and a big win among all names in K-pop.
