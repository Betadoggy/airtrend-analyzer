Title: Rare whiskies, cognacs, and more at duty-free prices
Source: CNA
URL: https://cnaluxury.channelnewsasia.com/experiences/rare-whiskies-cognacs-and-more-duty-free-prices-241091
--------------------------------------------------

Connoisseurs of fine wines and spirits now have the opportunity to acquire some of the world's rarest and most premium alcohol at duty-free prices through the collaboration between Changi Airport Group and Lotte Duty Free. Now into its second edition, World of Wines and Spirits (WOWS) is an online portal featuring a curated selection of over 100 premium liquor products from more than 56 brands. This exclusive shopping experience is set to run until Feb 15, 2024.

The WOWS portal has a digital catalog showcasing labels like Bowmore, The Macallan, Martell and Hennessy. Importantly, all the items featured on this platform are exclusive to WOWS and subsequently, some of these products will be on display at Lotte Duty Free outlets at the airport.

To enhance the shopping experience, customers who are interested in a particular item on the WOWS website can whatsapp to +65 9725 3459 (Chat Only) for enquiries.
