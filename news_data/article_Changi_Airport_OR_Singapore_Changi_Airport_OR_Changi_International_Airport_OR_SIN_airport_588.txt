Title: Burger & Lobster Is Closing In Singapore – Enjoy 1-For-1 Lobster Roll From Now Until 10 Mar
Source: Ladyironchef.com
URL: https://www.ladyironchef.com/2025/03/burger-lobster-singapore-closing/
--------------------------------------------------

Burger & Lobster opened in Singapore to much fanfare back in 2019 and they have just announced the permanent closure of their outlet at Jewel Changi Airport on 10 March.

Burger & Lobster’s beginnings on a London rooftop started in 2011 by a group of 4 schoolmates with only three items on their menu: whole lobster, lobster roll, and a beef burger—all of which were priced at £20 each.

From now until 10 March, Burger & Lobster Singapore is having a Buy One, Get One FREE Lobster Roll promotion.

How to Redeem:

Dine-in only at Burger & Lobster Jewel Changi

Tell your waiter the secret code: ROLL ON to unlock the special menu

Order one Lobster Roll & get another on the house!
