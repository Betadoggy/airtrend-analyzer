Title: Anwar arrives in Singapore for one-day working visit
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2023/09/13/anwar-arrives-in-singapore-for-one-day-working-visit
--------------------------------------------------

SINGAPORE: Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim has arrived in Singapore for a compact one-day working visit.

The aircraft carrying the Prime Minister, who is accompanied by his wife, Datuk Seri Dr Wan Azizah Wan Ismail, landed at the VIP Complex, Changi International Airport, at 10.30am on Wednesday (Sept 13).
