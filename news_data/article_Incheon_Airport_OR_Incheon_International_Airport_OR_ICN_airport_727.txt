Title: Ryu Seon Jae Fever — Byeon Woo Seok Experiences Heated Response In Taiwan
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/byeon-woo-seok-popularity-taiwan-crowd-airport/
--------------------------------------------------

Actor Byeon Woo Seok is the current K-Drama heartthrob. He gained fame through Lovely Runner, captivating hearts as the sweet and charming Ryu Seon Jae.

Timed perfectly is his Asia fanmeeting, SUMMER LETTER.

He left South Korea for Taiwan on June 6, 2024. The star was greeted warmly by his Taiwanese fans.

The crowd of fans stretched as far as the eye could see.

Thankfully, fans were kept at bay with the use of security barriers, giving Byeon Woo Seok ample space to move around.

He was totally surprised by the hot response from Taiwanese fans. It looks like he did not expect that many to show up at the airport to receive him!

One fan even brought a Seon Jae standee to the airport. Their dedication certainly caught Byeon Woo Seok’s eye. He approached them to give them a special heart sign in return for their love.

Fans couldn’t believe how cute he was. He made sure to greet the reporters with hearts and big smiles.

It looks like Byeon Woo Seok is still unused to his newfound fame. He was also recently mobbed at Incheon airport during his departure. Read more about it below.
