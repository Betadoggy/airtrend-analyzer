Title: Countries evacuate citizens as Israel-Hamas war rages
Source: The Times of India
URL: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/countries-evacuate-citizens-as-israel-hamas-war-rages/articleshow/104334639.cms
--------------------------------------------------

Synopsis

Several countries have started evacuation operations to repatriate their citizens from Israel and the Palestinian territories due to the ongoing violence. Argentina has already started evacuating over 1,200 citizens, while Brazil plans to mobilize at least six planes. Nigeria has airlifted over 300 citizens who were on a Christian pilgrimage.
