Title: SEVENTEEN Mingyu's Behavior Toward Fans Despite Being Mobbed At The Airport
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/seventeen-mingyu-behavior-mobbed-airport/

SEVENTEEN‘s Mingyu is being praised for his behavior towards fans despite being mobbed at the airport.

On June 12, Mingyu departed from Incheon Airport to go to Paris, France. As expected, the idol looked handsome as he adorned Calvin Klein, with many saying he was attending an event for the brand.

When Mingyu arrived, it wasn’t surprising that fans were waiting to greet the idol.

Netizens couldn’t hide their anger seeing the crowds “mobbing” the idol as soon as he stepped off the plane. Despite the crowds of fans, Mingyu continued to sign albums and gained attention after some fans almost hurt themselves because they didn’t see a fence, but the idol warned them. In particular, he personally pushed them back so they wouldn’t get hurt.

When the video was posted, netizens praised Mingyu for wanting to protect fans despite being mobbed at the airport.
