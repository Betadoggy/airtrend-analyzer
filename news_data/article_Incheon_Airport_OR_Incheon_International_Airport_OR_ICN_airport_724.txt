Title: IU Lands In Manila And Has The Sweetest Surprise For Filipino Fans
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/iu-manila-surprise-filipino-fans/
--------------------------------------------------

K-Pop’s beloved soloist IU has touched down in Manila ahead of her June 1 concert! The singer, known for her charming personality and iconic songs, was warmly greeted by a large crowd upon her arrival at both Incheon International Airport…

…and Ninoy Aquino International Airport. The concert, set to take place at the Philippine Arena in Bulacan, marks her first performance in the country in five years.

To express her gratitude and affection, IU prepared the sweetest surprise for her Filipino fans! She gifted the Uaenas waiting at the airport with an assortment of delicious Korean snacks, including treats from HBAF and L-GA.

However, what was meant to be a sweet gesture turned bittersweet when not all fans showed the same level of consideration. Some fans flocked to the snacks and hoarded them, leaving others without any.

Despite these hiccups, IU’s thoughtful gesture made the occasion even more memorable. Her kind act showed her genuine appreciation for her fans and added a special touch to her visit.
