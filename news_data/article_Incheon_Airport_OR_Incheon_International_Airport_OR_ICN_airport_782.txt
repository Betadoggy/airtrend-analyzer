Title: Aespa’s Karina Gains Attention For Her “Punk Rock” Styling At The Airport

The girls of aespa made their way to Incheon Airport on June 23, 2023, for a flight to Indonesia. They were scheduled to perform the Jakarta leg of their world tour, SYNK : HYPER LINE.

The airport was a crowded affair that day, with both Girls’ Generation’s Taeyeon and IVE flying out for their own concert tours. Despite the crowd, Karina kept her cool, posing for photos on the way.

Her makeup was as flawless as usual! The makeup artist placed emphasis on her lashes, keeping the rest of the face minimal.

She looked like the best blend between lovely and cool. While her makeup brought out her feminine features, her outfit had a punk rock twist.

Karina wore a vintage rock shirt with a rip at the collar.

She paired it with see-through knee-high socks and walkers.

The silver hoops in her ear added to the chic vibes.

The star also carried a small pink Chanel handbag.

Her outfit is the perfect balance of “Salty and Sweet!”

Fans couldn’t get enough of her look.

She sure looked like a rockstar.
