Title: Jennie’s Not In Paris But She’s Dressing For Fashion Week
Source: British Vogue
URL: https://www.vogue.co.uk/article/jennie-kim-chanel-paris-fashion-week
--------------------------------------------------

Chanel will this week unveil its spring/summer 2025 collection – the first to have been fully designed without a creative lead since Virginie Viard exited the maison in June. Succession rumours have abounded in her absence. Could Hedi Slimane – who has been reported to be leaving Celine for months – take up the mantle? How about the New York-favourite Thom Browne? Or is Jeremy Scott – who was earlier this summer spotted apartment hunting in Paris – the most logical heir to Karl Lagerfeld’s poptimism?

Simon Porte Jacquemus seems to be the name on everyone’s lips, but Chanel is in no rush to state a replacement. It has, after all, a number of visible figureheads who can for now plug the publicity hole. See: Jennie Kim – the one-time Jacquemus model – who was this weekend photographed at Incheon airport in a collarless jacket and hot pants with quilted Mary Janes and a jumbo cross-body all from Chanel. She will be among a number of representatives at tomorrow morning’s presentation in Paris.

Kim’s ties to the brand run deep. She was Lagerfeld’s last chosen muse before his death in 2019 and starred in several high-profile advertising campaigns during Viard’s tenure. This relationship has proven a boon to her wardrobe, thanks to the reinterpretation of look 164 from Lagerfeld’s autumn/winter 1990 collection she wore to the 2023 Met Gala and the lace-embroidered bridal gown she modelled at the 2023 Cannes Film Festival… not to mention all the pearls, tweeds and twinsets Jennie has worn on stage with Blackpink. What’s a creative director when there is already a “Human Chanel” in existence?
