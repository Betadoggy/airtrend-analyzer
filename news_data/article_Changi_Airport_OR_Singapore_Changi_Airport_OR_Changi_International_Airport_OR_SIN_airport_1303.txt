Title: SQ Stewardess Approaches Hebe Tien For A Chat At Changi Airport
Source: 8days.sg
URL: https://www.8days.sg/entertainment/local/hebe-tien-singapore-airlines-sq-stewardess-changi-airport-chat-821441
--------------------------------------------------

A perk of being a cabin crew is getting to meet overseas celebs flying in to or out of Singapore.

One very lucky Singapore Airline air stewardess got the chance to get really up close with Taiwanese star Hebe Tien at Changi Airport a few days back.

Recently, a fan shared on Facebook a clip she had taken of Hebe, 40, who was in town over the weekend for the One Love Asia Festival Singapore 2023.

"Would you dare to look into Hebe Tien's eyes? I don't because my soul will be hooked," she gushed. Um... what does she think Hebe is?

While the fan wouldn't have dared to get so close to the star, one SQ stewardess did not hold back.

In the video which appears to be taken from outside the arrival hall, Hebe, who was wearing a mask, was patiently waiting for her luggage with her crew inside when an SQ stewardess approached her.

Though we couldn't hear what the two were chatting about, the former S.H.E member could be seen smiling and nodding at the air stewardess, while casually running her fingers through her hair throughout the conversation.

To be honest, if you weren't aware that that's Hebe, you probably wouldn't think she's one of Asia's most popular singers.

But the massive crowd waiting outside the arrival hall for Hebe sure knew who she was and we guess the stewardess was definitely feeling more than just 'A Little Happiness' in that moment.
