Title: Anwar arrives in Singapore for 12th annual leaders' retreat
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/12/04/anwar-arrives-in-singapore-for-12th-annual-leaders039-retreat
--------------------------------------------------

SINGAPORE: Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim arrived here Thursday (Dec 4) for a one-day working visit to attend the 12th Malaysia-Singapore Leaders’ Retreat.

The aircraft carrying the Prime Minister touched down at the VIP Complex of Changi International Airport at 10.39am.
