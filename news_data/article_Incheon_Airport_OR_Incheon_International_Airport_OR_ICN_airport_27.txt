For this week only, United Airlines is offering discounted flights across its network, with special deals available to United Chase cardholders.

Flights are available to more than 30 destinations — including Japan, Australia, Thailand and Mexico — starting at 12,000 miles one-way for cardholders.

Deal basics

Airline: United.

Routes: 31 destinations across the United network.

How to book: Online with United for cardholders.

Travel dates: Jan. 12 to May 6, 2026. Exact availability depends on the route. Please note that blackout dates apply, and fares are subject to availability. Flights to Japan must be completed by March 14 and flights to Adelaide, Australia, by March 26.

Booking window: By Friday, Dec. 5, 2025.
