Title: 'Like a military operation': Koreans narrate horror stories of US immigration raid
Source: The Times of India
URL: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/like-a-military-operation-koreans-narrate-horror-stories-of-us-immigration-raid/articleshow/123862130.cms
--------------------------------------------------

Synopsis

South Korean workers returned home after being detained in the US. They described a raid at a car battery plant in Georgia. US authorities arrested workers on visa issues. Detainees reported poor conditions. Families expressed relief and anger. Some fear career repercussions. The incident strained relations between South Korea and the US.
