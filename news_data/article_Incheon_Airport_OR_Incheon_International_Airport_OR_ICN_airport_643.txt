During its first week, it secured the second spot in the weekly Top 10, amassing an impressive 72.1 million hours of viewership.

Fans of the Netflix series "XO, Kitty" have reason to celebrate as the show is set to return for its second season. Created by Jenny Han, the show serves as a spin-off of the popular "To All The Boys" film series, focusing on Kitty Song Covey's journey to find love in South Korea. Season 1 made its debut on Netflix on May 18, 2023, and now viewers are eager to find out what's next for Kitty and her complicated love life. Here's everything we know about "XO, Kitty" Season 2.

Season 2 Renewal: Anticipation and Production Hurdles

In June 2023, Netflix officially renewed "XO, Kitty" for a second season. The renewal wasn't surprising given the show's impressive debut. In its first week, it ranked as the second most-watched series in the weekly Top 10, with an impressive 72.1 million hours viewed.

However, the renewal came at an unusual time. It occurred amidst a Hollywood writers' strike, which has now ended, but negotiations between the Screen Actors Guild and the studios are still ongoing. This may cause delays in the production timeline.

Sang Heon Lee, who portrays Minho in the series, shed light on the situation, explaining that the strike made it challenging for the writers to conclude the script. He emphasized the need for time to navigate the scriptwriting process:

"The Writers’ Strike in Hollywood is going on, and because of the strike happening at the moment, it's not exactly easy for them to just reach a conclusion on how to approach and write the script. It doesn’t happen like that, so until the strike is over, we have to wait until they all just figure out the conclusion. And then, after that, they'll write a script."

Now that the strike is over, scriptwriting for "XO, Kitty" Season 2 is expected to commence, and if filming starts in 2024, the release may align with the timeline of Season 1. Production for the first season took place from March to June 2022 in various locations, including Seoul, Incheon Airport, and the Independence Hall of Korea in Cheonan. Following the same time frame, Season 2 could likely be released in 2025 at the earliest.

Season 2 Cast: Returning and Potential Cameos

The cast of "XO, Kitty" is a mix of new faces and familiar characters from the "To All The Boys" series. Given the cliffhanger ending of Season 1, it's safe to assume that most characters from the first season will return.

Anna Cathcart, who plays Kitty Song Covey, will lead the returning cast. Joining her are a group of talented Korean actors, including Minyeong Choi as Dae Heon Kim, Gia Kim as Yuri Han, Sang Heon Lee as Min Ho, Yunjin Kim as KISS school principal Jina Lim, and Michael K. Lee as Professor Lee.

John Corbett and Saraya Blue, who portray Dan Covey (Kitty's dad) and Trina (his girlfriend) from "To All The Boys," are also expected to make a return, offering guidance to Kitty.

There's been speculation about potential cameos by Lana Condor (Lara Jean) and Noah Centineo (Peter Kavinsky) from the "To All The Boys" series, but nothing has been confirmed. In an interview with The Wrap, Lana Condor hinted at the possibility of returning, stating, "I would definitely never say never. I mean, I think it’s pretty clear that I love the 'To All the Boys' world."

What to Expect in Season 2

Season 1 of "XO, Kitty" left Kitty Song Covey in a whirlwind of emotions. She successfully arrived in Seoul, only to be expelled from the Korean Independent School of Seoul (KISS) after it was discovered that she had been living in the boys' dormitory. Her relationship with Dae Heon Kim took a hit when she confessed her feelings for Yuri. However, Kitty discovered that Yuri had reunited with her girlfriend, Juliana. To add complexity to the situation, Kitty found herself seated next to Min Ho on her flight back to Los Angeles, and he confessed his feelings for her.

In an interview with Netflix, Anna Cathcart teased what lies ahead for Kitty in Season 2, suggesting that her journey of self-discovery will continue:

"By the end, she’s like, ‘I literally have no idea about anything. I am figuring it out. There are so many new people now that are a possibility, to see her world expand is a huge, huge difference and will be a really fun thing to follow."

The burning question for viewers is who Kitty will end up with in Season 2. Whether her relationship with Dae will continue after a heartbreaking yet honest breakup remains uncertain.

Minyeong Choi, who portrays Dae, shared insights into his character, emphasizing that Dae believes letting Kitty go is the way to love her. Sang Heon Lee, who plays Min Ho, hinted that his character is still in love with Kitty, raising the possibility of romance between the two friends.

Kitty's return to South Korea, which might be facilitated by Yuri's plea to reconsider her expulsion, could set the stage for complex developments in her relationships. The quest to uncover the identity of Kitty's mom, Eve Song, is also a storyline on the horizon for Season 2.

As fans eagerly await the return of "XO, Kitty," they can relish the thought of more romantic entanglements, character growth, and unexpected twists. Season 1 is available for streaming on Netflix.

FAQs

Where can I watch XO, Kitty season 2?

Is KISS a real school in Korea?

Behind the idyllic backdrop of the romantic comedy series, the Korean Independent School of Seoul featured in "XO, Kitty" is likely a fictional creation.
