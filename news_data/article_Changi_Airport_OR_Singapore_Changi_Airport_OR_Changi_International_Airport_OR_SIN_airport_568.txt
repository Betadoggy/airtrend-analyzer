Title: As good neighbours, Malaysia and Singapore must resolve water supply issues, says Anwar
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2024/06/12/as-good-neighbours-malaysia-and-singapore-must-resolve-water-supply-issues-says-anwar
--------------------------------------------------

PUTRAJAYA: Malaysia and Singapore have agreed to accelerate finding resolutions on outstanding issues such as the water agreement and maritime boundary delimitation, says Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim.

The Prime Minister said issues related to water agreement are complex and should be resolved amicably and should not deter both countries from exploring new avenues.
