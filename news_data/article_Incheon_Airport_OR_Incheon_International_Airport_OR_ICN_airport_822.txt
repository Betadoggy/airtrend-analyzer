Title: BLACKPINK's Rosé Joins The Bold "Underwear" Fashion Trend With See-Through Dress At Saint Laurent Show
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/blackpink-rose-joins-bold-underwear-fashion-trend-sheer-dress-saint-laurent/
--------------------------------------------------

As an ambassador for the luxury French fashion brand Saint Laurent since 2020, BLACKPINK‘s Rosé recently attended the designer’s Fall/Winter 2024-2025 show in Paris.

The BLACKPINK members are global “it girls,” frequently setting and following trends and often becoming “sold-out queens” when they influence sales of the items they wear and the products they use.

Ahead of the Saint Laurent show, Rosé was already stealing hearts with her trendy fashion as she made her way to France from Korea.

The Saint Laurent show took place on February 27, and Rosé stunned netizens by joining the rising “underwear” fashion trend that has recently made its way to Korea.

Veteran singer Lee Hyori sported the trend last spring, LE SSERAFIM‘s Yunjin and Chaewon recently sported the trend in their Good Bones teaser, and actress Moon Ga Young wore the trend at the Dolce & Gabbana show during Milan Fashion Week.

For her front-row look for Paris Fashion Week, Rosé kept with the sheer theme of Saint Laurent’s new collection and looked incredible in a see-through dress with a bralette and high-waisted underwear underneath.

The BLACKPINK member kept her accessories simple, donning a pair of her iconic dark sunglasses to complete the look.

Netizens are loving Rosé’s effortlessly gorgeous and bold fashion look!
