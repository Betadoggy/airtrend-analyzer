Title: Conrad Singapore Orchard Now Accepting Reservations
Source: Hospitality Net

Nestled in a coveted enclave along vibrant Orchard Road, Conrad Singapore Orchard is an urban oasis with world-class contemporary art and abundant natural light that harmonises with Singapore's tropical greenery through the soaring 12-storey atrium.

Originally envisioned by renowned American architect John Portman, the distinctive atrium hotel features a skylit lobby that springs to life with illuminated glass elevator cabs that serve as mesmerising kinetic art. Prominent sculptures adorn the hotel, including Stephanie Scuris' welcoming Harmony Fountain at the entrance and Michio Ihara's dazzling Singapore Shower.

All 445 gracefully appointed rooms, including 46 suites with spacious double terraces, are bathed in natural light and radiate a sophisticated residential ambience achieved through thoughtful design. Rooms are generously sized at 36 square metres, while suite categories range from 81 to 210 square metres.

Additional amenities include a pet-friendly programme, a sanctuary-like executive lounge, a fully equipped fitness centre, and an outdoor pool sheltered amidst lush landscaping, complemented by over 1,500 square metres of event spaces and a portfolio of incredible dining experiences. The hotel is conveniently located within a half-hour's drive from Singapore Changi Airport, offering cultured travellers swift access to the city's heartbeat of refined living experiences and world-class attractions, including the Singapore Botanic Gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
