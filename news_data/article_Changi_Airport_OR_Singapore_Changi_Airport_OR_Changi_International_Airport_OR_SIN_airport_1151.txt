Title: Harbour Rights sells to SBS in Oz, Hong Kong’s TVB and picks up Kiwi series
Source: C21media.net
URL: https://www.c21media.net/news/harbour-rights-sells-to-sbs-in-oz-hong-kongs-tvb-and-picks-up-kiwi-series/
--------------------------------------------------

Broadcasters SBS in Australia and TVB in Hong Kong have acquired factual content from distributor Harbour Rights, including docs on classic sci-fi movies and the Second World War and culinary series.

SBS has picked up Science Fiction Revolution (5×52’), which explores the sci-fi movies of the 80s, plus Second World War docs Shooter of War (1×56’) and The Incredible Life of Captain Harding Leaf (1×52’).

TVB has acquired the latest three episodes of food series Masters of Savours, comprising Masters of Chilies (1×52’), Masters of Seaweed (1×52’) and Masters of Lemon (1×52’).

Meanwhile, Harbour Rights has added two New Zealand-produced series to its catalogue. Kai Safari (66×26’) is a food- and outdoors-themed series, while Super Kura (12×26’) follows a community that created and now runs the first preschool for Maori disabled children.

The Hong Kong-based distributor has also acquired Lost Waterfronts (2×48’), produced by Singapore’s Sitting in Pictures, which demonstrates how Singapore’s eastern and southern coastlines make room for places like Marine Parade, Changi Airport and Marina Bay Sands.
