Title: Crypto elite shun NFTs to bet on 69-million-year-old dinosaur fossils
Source: The Times of India
URL: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/crypto-elite-shun-nfts-to-bet-on-69-million-year-old-dinosaur-fossils/articleshow/125996302.cms
--------------------------------------------------

Synopsis

A 69-million-year-old triceratops fossil worth $5 million now resides in a Singapore vault. This purchase by crypto investors signals a shift away from digital art. The crypto elite are now investing in tangible assets like antiques and gold. This trend suggests a move towards traditional stores of value for many in the crypto market.
