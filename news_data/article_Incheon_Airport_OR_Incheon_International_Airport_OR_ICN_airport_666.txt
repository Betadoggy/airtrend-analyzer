Title: Shohei Ohtani's wife, Mamiko Tanaka, remains out of the public eye in the postseason, raising questions
Source: Marca
URL: https://www.marca.com/en/mlb/los-angeles-dodgers/2024/10/18/671264cfca4741bf278b4574.html
--------------------------------------------------

Shohei Ohtani's first postseason run with the Los Angeles Dodgers has captivated fans, as the two-way superstar continues to showcase the skills that made him a baseball phenomenon. Ohtani, who became the first player ever to hit 50 home runs and steal 50 bases in a single season, remains a key figure in the Dodgers' playoff success.

In Game 3 of the National League Championship Series (NLCS) against the New York Mets, Ohtani sent shockwaves through Citi Field with a powerful 410-foot home run, helping the Dodgers to an 8-0 victory.

He followed that up with another impressive blast in Game 4 on October 17, launching a 422-foot homer that gave the Dodgers an early lead and marked his first postseason hit without runners on base.

Out of the public eye

While Ohtani's performance on the field has been grabbing headlines, there's been just as much curiosity off the field regarding his wife, Mamiko Tanaka.

The 27-year-old, who married Ohtani in February 2024, has stayed largely out of the public eye, preferring to maintain a low profile.

Tanaka, a former professional basketball player in Japan, made her mark as a forward for the Fujitsu Red Wave before stepping away from the sport.

When Ohtani first announced their marriage, he chose to keep her identity under wraps, only revealing her name later when he posted a photo of the two together just before a trip to South Korea.

Despite the fanfare that awaited them at Incheon International Airport, Tanaka stayed in the background, walking behind Ohtani and keeping her presence understated.

The former athlete seems to be comfortable staying out of the limelight. Though she's rarely seen in the public eye, she occasionally makes quiet appearances to support her husband. In May, the couple was spotted mingling with singer Ed Sheeran at the Los Angeles Dodgers Foundation Blue Diamond Gala, marking one of their few public outings together.
