Title: Changi Jurassic Mile in Singapore
Source: Atlas Obscura
URL: https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/changi-jurassic-mile-singapore
--------------------------------------------------

Stretching over one kilometer, Changi Jurassic Mile is an interesting outdoor attraction located outside the Terminal 4 of Singapore's Changi Airport.

The trail showcases a diverse range of dinosaur species, including the iconic Tyrannosaurus rex, towering Brachiosaurus, and the Stegosaurus. Interestingly, the trail is created with the idea of visitors walking in a proper Dinosasur Park, with warning signs all around, and dinosaurs trying to escape their cages or attacking golf carts.

The trail is entirely outdoor, and can be experienced either by foot or bicycle.

The mile starts right outside Terminal 4 of Changi airport. Just follow the signs when outside, close to the external wall of the golf course. The trail is open 24h and free, just keep in mind that during the day it can be extremely hot.
