I’ve been to all but a few of them, and I can attest to the fact that this list is pretty spot on—except for CDG and FCO. While some parts of those airports might be great, overall, I don’t think frequent travelers would consider Paris or Rome’s main airports their favorites.

My personal favorite airports in the world are Singapore Changi Airport, followed by Hong Kong International Airport and Hamad International Airport in Doha.

There are some airports that rightfully didn’t make the list like Los Angeles (LAX), which I agree is a complete mess but with so much potential. That said, I do love the Tom Bradley International Terminal (TBIT or Terminal B), except for the remote gates, which feel like an eternity to walk to and from. Fortunately, LAX is undergoing major improvements as the city prepares to welcome visitors for global events like the FIFA World Cup (2026) and the Summer Olympics (2028). If you’re interested in what improvements are being made to LAX, Los Angeles World Airports (LAWA) has all of the airport’s capital improvement projects listed here.
