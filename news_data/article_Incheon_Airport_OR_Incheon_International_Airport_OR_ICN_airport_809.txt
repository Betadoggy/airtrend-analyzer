Title: Han So Hee Earns Praise For Her Behavior Toward Fans
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/han-so-hee-earns-praise-behavior-toward-fans/
--------------------------------------------------

Han So Hee recently returned from Paris after visiting the capital of France for an event with Boucheron.

Han So Hee was officially named Boucheron’s first Korean global ambassador on June 26, 2023, and attended her first official event as their ambassador.

While in Paris, and upon her return to Korea, Han So Hee showcased her true personality during her numerous heartwarming interactions with fans.

One lucky fan shared their experience of running into Han So Hee in Paris and recounted their five-minute meeting. The fan called Han So Hee “the kindest” and told the talented actress that fans are excited about her upcoming music video with BTS‘s Jungkook.

Han So Hee not only took time to greet people, but she sweetly accepted gifts thoughtfully prepared for her by fans and signed items when she could.

Netizens took notice of Han So Hee’s fan service, praising her behavior and kind personality while pointing out additional moments the actress took time to address, acknowledge, and thank fans.

After arriving back home, Han So Hee again displayed her thoughtful personality. Fans eagerly waited for Han So Hee to arrive, and the actress went out of her way to collect gifts and letters from fans.

After receiving letters and gifts, Han So Hee held them close to her and made sure they were safely placed in a bag while she was escorted toward her vehicle.

Netizens and fans praised Han So Hee’s consistently kind and caring personality.
