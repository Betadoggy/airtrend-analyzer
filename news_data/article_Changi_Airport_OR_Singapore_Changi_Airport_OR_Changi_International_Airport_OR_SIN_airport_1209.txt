Title: Firefly expands Subang jet operations with the launch of new direct routes to Kuching and Singapore
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/02/07/firefly-expands-subang-jet-operations-with-the-launch-of-new-direct-routes-to-kuching-and-singapore
--------------------------------------------------

SEPANG: Firefly, a subsidiary of the Malaysia Aviation Group (MAG), announced the expansion of its jet operations from Sultan Abdul Aziz Shah Airport, Subang (SZB) with the launch of two new direct flights to Kuching and Changi International Airport beginning March 24.

Travellers can look forward to seamless connectivity between Subang and two highly sought after destinations - Kuching and Singapore.
