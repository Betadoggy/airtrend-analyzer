Meanwhile, jury chair Siew Ting Foo, global head of brand and insights at HP, bestowed the coveted Chair’s Award upon ‘It’s Time to Play Better,’ created by Hopeful Monsters for Park in Australia. Motivated by the eye-watering stat that 96% of women have had to play football in a men’s kit at some point in their career, the campaign amplified the voices of female players worldwide to challenge the status quo and overcome the all-too-common problem of ill-fitting kits for women.

Innovative tactics, including a ‘call to arms’ hero video featuring A-League players and strategic (and unorthodox) media placements, garnered widespread attention and sparked a global movement.

The Drum Awards for Marketing APAC is the first in a three-legged global tour celebrating the world’s best marketing over the next three weeks. The Drum Awards for Marketing Americas takes place on Wednesday, June 5, and The Drum Awards for Marketing EMEA on Thursday, June 13. All events will be streamed live and on-demand on thedrum.com.
