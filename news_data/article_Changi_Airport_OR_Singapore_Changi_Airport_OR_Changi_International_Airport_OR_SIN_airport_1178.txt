Title: Anwar arrives in Singapore for Shangri-La dialogue
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/05/31/anwar-arrives-in-singapore-for-shangri-la-dialogue
--------------------------------------------------

SINGAPORE: Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim has arrived in Singapore for a one-day working visit to participate in the 22nd Shangri-La Dialogue (SLD), the premier defence and security conference in the Asia-Pacific region.

The aircraft carrying the Prime Minister touched down at Singapore's Changi Airport at 11.39am Saturday (May 31).
