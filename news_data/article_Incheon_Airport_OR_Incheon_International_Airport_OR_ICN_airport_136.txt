Title: BTS’s Jin Breaks His Tie With Blackpink With A New Top 10 Bestseller
Source: Forbes
URL: https://www.forbes.com/sites/hughmcintyre/2025/02/10/btss-jin-breaks-his-tie-with-blackpink-with-a-new-top-10-bestseller/
--------------------------------------------------

Jin is back inside the top 10 on not one, but two different Billboard charts this week. His latest single “Close to You” succeeds almost exclusively thanks to an early and impressive sales count, one which helps him break out of a tie with another one of South Korea's greatest musical exports.

“Close to You” launches at No. 9 on the Digital Song Sales chart this week. The tune marks Jin’s sixth solo appearance inside the highest tier on Billboard's ranking of the bestselling tracks throughout the country.

With a sixth top 10 to his credit, Jin passes Blackpink on the list of the most successful South Korean artists of all time on the Digital Song Sales chart--at least when looking specifically at those musicians that have accrued the most top 10s. Before “Close To You” arrived, he was matched with the girl group with five top 10s apiece, but now he's passed them.

Among all K pop favorites, BTS leads the charge with a historic 36 top 10 smashes. One of its members, Jung Kook, is the only soloist to also pass double-digit top 10s, as he has now collected 14 appearances between Nos. 1 and 10 on his own.

In between BTS and Jung Kook and Jin with his six smashes come five other K-pop powerhouses. Amazingly, all of them are members of the South Korean septet, as all of them have found great success on their own as well as with BTS.

Jimin and Suga, who also releases music under the name Agust D, are tied for the third-most top 10 of all time on the Digital Song Sales chart among K-pop musicians, with nine apiece. J-Hope comes next with just one fewer smash. Both RM and V sit side by side, one rung ahead of Jin, with seven top 10s apiece.

“Close to You” sold just under 3,000 copies across America in its debut tracking frame, according to Luminate. That sum is large enough to ensure that not only does Jin debut inside the top 10 on the all-genre, all-language Digital Song Sales chart, but that he conquers the World Digital Song Sales ranking as well. On that list, he lands his seventh No. 1 and eleventh top 10 on his own.
