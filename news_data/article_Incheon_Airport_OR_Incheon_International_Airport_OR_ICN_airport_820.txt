Text description provided by the architects. A microcosm of a neighborhood created by rooms, yards and hills. Cheongna District is a new city development, located near Incheon, Korea, on land reclaimed from the sea. All the facilities in Cheongna are vehicle-centered, making it difficult to find a human scale. Children in this area grow up in an urban environment, comprised mainly of apartments and office buildings, where convenience facilities are fragmentary and where both the streets and the natural environment, which should be interwoven together, are somewhat insufficient.
