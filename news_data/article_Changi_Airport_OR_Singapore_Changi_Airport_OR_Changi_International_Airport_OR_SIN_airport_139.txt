It’s Sunday again, so it must be time for some more Airplane Art.

This week, we have a Cathay Pacific Airbus A350-900 at Hong Kong International Airport as it awaits its next journey to Singapore Changi Airport.

Cathay Pacific s operates a fleet of 29 Airbus A350-900. They also operate the Airbus A330-300, Airbus A350-1000, and Boeing 777-300ER in its passenger wide-body operation. The airline also operates the Boeing 747-400 and Boeing 747-8F in its cargo wing.

Together, they connect Hong Kong to the world.

The depicted is B-LRS. It joined the Cathay Pacific fleet in August 2017 and has remained in the fleet since then. At the time of writing, the aircraft is active, operating out of its Hong Kong home to San Francisco, London Heathrow, Singapore and Tokyo-Narita

More Airplane Art next week.
