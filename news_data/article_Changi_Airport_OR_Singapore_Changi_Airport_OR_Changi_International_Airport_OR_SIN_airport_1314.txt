Title: K-Pop Idol From Boyband Vixx Gives Fans Krispy Kreme Donuts At Changi Airport
Source: 8days.sg
URL: https://www.8days.sg/entertainment/local/vixx-ken-gives-fans-krispy-kreme-donuts-changi-airport-847156
--------------------------------------------------

It wasn’t just selfies and waves that fans of Ken from K-pop boyband Vixx got when they turned up at Changi Airport to send him off on Sunday (Sep 1) — the star also handed out Krispy Kreme donuts.

The 33-year-old singer, also known as Lee Jae Hwan, was in Singapore for a meet-and-greet the day before.

But before flying out, he decided to give fans one last treat — literally.

In a video posted by a fan on Xiaohongshu, Ken is seen buying donuts from a Krispy Kreme outlet at Terminal 3, before handing them out to the group of fans eagerly filming videos of him.

One fan thought she had missed out, until Ken turned back as he was walking off to give her the last two glazed donuts.

Now, isn’t that the sweetest send-off from an idol?
