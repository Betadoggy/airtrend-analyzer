Title: Han So Hee's "Couple Ring" Sparks New Round Of Speculation
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/han-so-hee-ryu-jun-yeol-ring/
--------------------------------------------------

Han So Hee‘s ring is at the center of the latest speculation surrounding the actress and Ryu Jun Yeol.

On March 18, Han So Hee arrived at Incheon Airport after completing her eventful trip to Hawaii.

Han So Hee Makes Her First Public Appearance Following Dating Controversy With Ryu Jun Yeol

When Han So Hee entered the main part of Incheon Airport, she was unsurprisingly very subdued, and the small crowds probably came as a relief to the actress. Walking hand in hand with her staff, Han So Hee started walking towards the exit.

Photos of the star’s arrival quickly went viral, with many focusing their attention on one of her accessories. In the photos, Han So Hee can be seen wearing a ring on her ring finger.

Immediately, netizens claimed that the ring was a couple ring and that the actress was flaunting her new relationship with Ryu Jun Yeol. This, however, was debunked when, on March 19, OSEN reported that the ring was a friendship ring.

After confirming with Han So Hee, the ring on her fourth finger is a friendship ring. It’s a birthstone ring made with her friends from her hometown.
