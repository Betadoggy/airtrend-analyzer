Title: Lady Gaga Spotted At Changi Airport Clearing Security Check Before Flying To Dubai On Emirates
Source: 8days.sg
URL: https://www.8days.sg/entertainment/local/lady-gaga-changi-airport-clearing-security-check-dubai-emirates-843856
--------------------------------------------------

After wrapping up her four-night Mayhem World Tour stop in Singapore, American pop star Lady Gaga was spotted at Changi Airport undergoing a security check at a boarding gate C1

The 39-year-old was seen by Xiaohongshu user Eva getting ready to board an Emirates flight to Dubai that left on May 25 at 140am.

Wait, Lady Gaga flew commercial and not on a private jet?

According to Eva, who is a fan of the singer, she was wandering around the airport when she recognised Lady Gaga’s fiancé, Michael Polansky, then spotting the Gaga singer beside him.

Dressed in a black form-fitting outfit and towering platforms, Lady Gaga navigated the security check without an entourage.

Eva couldn’t believe her eyes and said she had to use her phone to zoom into Lady Gaga to confirm that it was her.

Photos in the comments indicated that Lady Gaga’s flight was bound for Dubai.

While some fans were excited about the possibility of bumping into her in the UAE, others speculated that she was simply transiting before returning to the United States.
