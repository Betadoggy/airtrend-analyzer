Title: Byeon Woo Seok's Heartbreaking Change In Behavior At The Airport Amid Security Controversy
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/actor-byeon-woo-seok-behavior-heartbreaking-change-security-controversy/
--------------------------------------------------

Netizens are heartbroken after seeing Byeon Woo Seok‘s behavior at the airport amid his security controversy.

Recently, the actor’s security team’s “over-the-top” excessive behavior has spread to the point that it negatively impacts the actor. On July 16, Byeon Woo Seok made his way back to Korea following his overseas schedules.

A clip shared by a fan showed the actor making his way through the airport and, unsurprisingly, surrounded by fans. As he walked through, he was seen gently moving his manager’s hand down.

The clip was then slowed down. It shows that as Byeon Woo Seok’s manager slightly raised his hand, the actor pushed it down, seemingly to avoid anything that could be seen as “aggressive” towards fans, particularly considering the recent news and allegations against his security team.

When the clips were shared, netizens were heartbroken seeing Byeon Woo Seok’s actions. They realized how much the news and criticism are impacting him, in a way that means he is constantly conscious about his behavior and that of those around him when it comes to interacting with fans, even if it’s for his safety.

Even when arriving in Korea through Incheon Airport, despite the huge crowds, everything seemed more subdued.

Netizens stayed very close to get a glimpse or interaction with the actor, but those around the actor seemed wary and tried to find different ways to protect him.
