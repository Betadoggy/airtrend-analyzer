Title: Blackpink Singer Jennie Ties Jung Kook For A Very Special Chart Record
Source: Forbes
URL: https://www.forbes.com/sites/hughmcintyre/2024/02/23/blackpink-singer-jennie-ties-jung-kook-for-a-very-special-chart-record/
--------------------------------------------------

Blackpink singer Jennie continues to make her presence felt on the Billboard charts with her current single “One of the Girls,” which has been steadily climbing the rankings for several months. The tune shows some serious resilience on various lists, including the Rhythmic Airplay chart, where it experiences another boost this week.

As “One of the Girls” continues to improve its chart performance, Jennie achieves a significant milestone, tying an important record and etching her name in Billboard’s history books once again.

“One of the Girls” ascends to No. 13 on this week’s Rhythmic Airplay chart, moving up from its previous position at No. 16. This Billboard ranking tracks the songs that garner the largest audience across the United States each week, based on the number of impressions each song accumulates.

The number 13 proves to be special and incredibly lucky for “One of the Girls” on the Rhythmic Airplay chart, as it matches the all-time record for the highest-charting hit by any South Korean musician in the history of the tally. This placement puts Jennie on par with Jung Kook, one of the seven members of the K-pop boy band BTS.

Jung Kook set this reword a few months ago with his solo single “Too Much,” which also reached No. 13 on the same chart. “Too Much,” which is fronted by The Kid Laroi and which also features Central Cee, reached the position relatively recently after its release in October 2023.

As “One of the Girls” continues its upward trajectory, there’s a good chance that it could break its tie with Jung Kook in the coming days. With its current momentum, the track might soon become the first top 10 hit by a South Korean artist in the Rhythmic Airplay chart’s history.

Jennie is credited as a featured artist on “One of the Girls,” alongside Lily Rose Depp. The song was co-written and led by The Weeknd, who created it for his television series The Idol, which aired on HBO last year. Despite the show not being renewed for a second season, the music from the series has continued to thrive, helping stars like Jennie make history on a number of Billboard charts.
