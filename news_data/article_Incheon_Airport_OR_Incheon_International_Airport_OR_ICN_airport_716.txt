Title: Unwelcomed K-Drama for Malaysian woman who denied entry into South Korea
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2023/10/14/unwelcomed-k-drama-for-malaysian-woman-who-denied-entry-into-south-korea
--------------------------------------------------

PETALING JAYA: A long-anticipated holiday ended in heartbreak for Hazwani Amira, who was denied entry into South Korea.

Recounting her unfortunate experience on TikTok, Hazwani said she was forced to return because her just renewed passport lacked physical stamps from the Malaysian Immigration Department and that she did not bring along her old passport.
