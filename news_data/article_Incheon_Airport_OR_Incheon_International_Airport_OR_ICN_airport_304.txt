Title: Helsinki Airport world's 7th best in Travel + Leisure magazine ranking
Source: YLE News
URL: https://yle.fi/a/74-20173663
--------------------------------------------------

Helsinki Airport ranked seventh-best in the world, according to a survey published by Travel + Leisure magazine.

Helsinki Airport was also the only European destination that made it into the survey's top ten. This year also marked Helsinki Airport’s first appearance on the publication's annual best-of list.

Around 180,000 of the magazine's readers took part in the survey. Among other things, they were asked to rate airports' accessibility accommodations, check-in arrangements, eateries and design.

Helsinki Airport underwent a significant, decade-long renovation that wrapped up a couple of years ago. The main change was bringing check-in points, security checks and baggage claims facilities in a single terminal.

Travel + Leisure magazine's list

1. Istanbul Airport

2. Singapore Changi Airport

3. Hamad International Airport in Qatar

4. Zayed International Airport in UAE

5. Dubai International Airport

6. Hong Kong International Airport

7. Helsinki Airport, also known as Helsinki-Vantaa Airport

8. Haneda Airport in Tokyo

9. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport in Mumbai

10. Incheon International Airport in South Korea

'Gaining recognition'

The director at national airport operator Finavia, Ulla Serlenius, said the company is proud that the facility is gaining international recognition.

"Helsinki Airport has maintained its strong position among the best airports in the world, which increases Finland’s attractiveness as a destination," Serlenius said in a company press release.

"Airports are evaluated on the basis of factors such as accessibility, smooth passenger processes, restaurants, shops and design. The final placement is determined by an average of these evaluations," she said.
