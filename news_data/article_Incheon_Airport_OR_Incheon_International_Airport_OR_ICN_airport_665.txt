New Delhi, March 26, 2024 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- As per new research by Astute Analytica, the global airport baggage handling system market is projected to reach US$ 13.05 billion by 2032, up from US$ 7.30 billion in 2023, at a CAGR of 6.67% during the forecast period 2024–2032.

The incredible volume of air travel puts a massive strain on baggage handling systems worldwide. In 2022 alone, a staggering 4.5 billion bags were processed by airports globally. While the rate of mishandled baggage was relatively low at 5.57 bags for every thousand passengers, the sheer number of travelers still results in a significant number of mishandled bags. Issues with transfers account for approximately 45% of these cases. The good news is that the vast majority (97%) of mishandled luggage is eventually recovered, with only a tiny percentage (0.57%) ending up truly lost. Still, airlines face a $2.5 billion annual cost due to mishandled baggage.

As passenger numbers continue to climb, projected to double by 2037, efficient baggage handling system such as check-in systems, security screening systems, transportation, sortation and storage systems, and reclaim systems will become even more critical. By 2022, an estimated 26% of bags were expected to carry RFID chips, a technology with the potential to cut mishandling rates by up to 25%.

Security Remains Key Driver

Security remains a top priority, with 100% of checked luggage undergoing screening in many countries. To give passengers peace of mind, around 60% of airlines and airports introduced real-time baggage tracking systems by 2019. Artificial intelligence (AI) further bolsters efficiency, with more than 40% of airlines deploying AI-powered baggage handling systems by the end of 2022. Robotics also plays a growing role, with 42% of airports planning to incorporate robots into their processes by 2025. Sustainability joins efficiency and security as a central focus, with modern baggage systems contributing to a 10-15% reduction in airports' carbon footprints. These compelling statistics underscore the ever-increasing demand for advanced airport baggage handling systems to seamlessly manage the vast flow of luggage associated with global air travel.

The Lucrative Opportunity in Airport Baggage Handling System Market: A Data-Driven Perspective

The sheer volume of global air travel creates a pressing need for efficient, seamless baggage handling. In 2022 alone, airports managed an impressive 4.5 billion bags. The good news is that the rate of mishandled baggage was relatively low at 5.57 bags for every thousand passengers. Yet, these mishandlings still cost airlines a hefty $2.5 billion annually and cause passenger inconvenience. This underscores the demand for technologically-driven airport baggage handling systems, especially as passenger numbers are expected to double by 2037.

Modern airport baggage handling system market is characterized by speed and efficiency. Automated systems can move and sort bags at up to 10 meters per second, enabling major hubs to process upwards of 30,000 bags per hour, ensuring timely flight schedules. Moreover, technologies like RFID and barcode scanning provide nearly 100% tracking accuracy, minimizing lost luggage. In fact, 97% of initially mishandled bags are eventually returned to their owners. Redundancies within these systems provide reliability and keep operations flowing even if certain components experience issues.

Passenger satisfaction is deeply linked to efficient baggage handling. Automated systems ensure bags arrive promptly at their destination, creating a positive travel experience. Passengers also appreciate being freed from having to transport their own luggage after check-in. Within the airport, baggage systems streamline logistics by moving luggage swiftly between key areas like check-in, security, and baggage claim. These systems can be customized to any terminal layout and reduce the amount of space needed for baggage handling in passenger areas.

Airlines in the airport baggage handling system market also benefit from robust baggage handling systems, as reliability becomes a deciding factor when choosing hub airports. Furthermore, automation reduces labor costs and allows for round-the-clock operations. The data generated by these systems allows for optimization and improved planning, benefiting both airlines and airport operations overall.

Segmental Analysis: A Market Snapshot of Airport Baggage Handling

The airport baggage handling systems market is a diverse landscape, segmented by type, solution, airport class, and tracking technology. The traditional conveyor system reigns supreme, holding a 72.9% revenue share due to its robustness, scalability, and ease of integration. However, the destination-coded vehicle (DCV) segment shows promising growth (CAGR of 7.15%), offering more flexibility, reduced manual intervention, and lower mishandling rates.

Analyzing the airport baggage handling system market by solution, the Transportation, Sortation, and Storage Systems segment dominates with a 50.4% share. This reflects the fundamental need for efficient baggage movement, sorting, and temporary storage within all airports.

Class A mega-hub airports hold a 42.4% market share, processing vast volumes of baggage and prioritizing innovation due to their high traffic and financial capabilities. Interestingly, Class B airports are emerging as fast adopters with a CAGR of 7.06%, driven by increased passenger traffic and infrastructure investment in developing economies.

Barcode technology remains the preferred tracking method in 76.6% of airports due to its cost-effectiveness, reliability, and standardization. However, RFID technology is gaining traction with a projected CAGR of 6.93%, offering higher accuracy (99%), reduced mishandling, and decreasing costs. Industry leaders are already deploying RFID, and its adoption is expected to grow significantly in the coming years, potentially saving airlines billions of dollars.

Asia Pacific is an Epicenter of Global Airport Baggage Handling System Market

The Asia Pacific region has seen tremendous growth in tourism in recent years. Countries like China, Japan, South Korea, Thailand, and others have become increasingly popular destinations for international travelers, attracted by the region's rich cultural heritage, natural beauty, and growing business opportunities. In 2023, Japan is expected to host 32 million international tourists, while Cambodia anticipates welcoming 4 million visitors. To accommodate the surge in air travel, many countries in the Asia Pacific are investing heavily in airport infrastructure. New airports are being built and existing ones are undergoing expansions to increase capacity and improve facilities. For example, the newly constructed Beijing Daxing International Airport introduced RFID technology for baggage tracking. These infrastructure investments are driving demand for advanced baggage handling systems.

Airports in the Asia Pacific airport baggage handling system market are adopting cutting-edge technologies to enhance efficiency and passenger experience. This includes automated baggage handling systems, self-service check-in kiosks, biometric security systems, and more. In 2018, Siemens commissioned one of the world's most modern baggage handling systems at Incheon International Airport in South Korea, capable of processing nearly 20,000 bags per hour.

The Asia Pacific is home to some of the world's fastest-growing economies. Initiatives like China's Belt and Road are boosting connectivity and investment in airport development across the region. The growing affluence and business activity are fueling both leisure and business travel. As a result, airports are seeing higher passenger volumes and demand for efficient baggage handling. In line with this, the airport baggage handling systems market in Asia Pacific is moderately fragmented with several key players. Companies like John Bean Technologies, ADELTE Group, Cavotec, and others are competing for contracts with different airports in the region. This competitive landscape is driving innovation and investments in advanced baggage handling solutions.
