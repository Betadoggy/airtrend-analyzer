Title: Kim Seri denies fleeing to Japan, says ready to cooperate with authorities
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2024/12/20/kim-seri-denies-fleeing-to-japan-says-ready-to-cooperate-with-authorities
--------------------------------------------------

SHAH ALAM: Social media influencer Kim Seri has denied fleeing to Japan over allegations of a fraudulent travel package she managed.

Kim Seri, whose real name is Seri Azlina, said she is ready to cooperate with the authorities following police reports and almost half a million ringgit in losses linked to the Kim Seri tour package scandal, reports Sinar Harian.
