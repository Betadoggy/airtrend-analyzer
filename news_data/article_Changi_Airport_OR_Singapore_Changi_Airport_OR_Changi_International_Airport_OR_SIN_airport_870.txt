Title: How Changi Airport tapped into Swiftie fever with the beJEWELed experience
Source: The Drum
URL: https://www.thedrum.com/news/2024/05/30/how-changi-airport-tapped-swiftie-fever-with-the-bejeweled-experience
--------------------------------------------------

In March 2024, Taylor Swift, the world’s largest pop star, was scheduled to hold six concerts in Singapore. The anticipation surrounding Taylor Swift’s six concerts in Singapore sparked an 18% week-on-week increase in flight bookings from her dedicated fanbase, known as Swifties, across the region. With Singapore as the exclusive Southeast Asia destination, CAG recognized a strategic opportunity to capitalize on this cultural moment to create brand relevance with Swifties.

Objectives

For CAG, the challenge lay in transcending its identity as a mere airport by creating a compelling and memorable experience for Swifties at Changi. The objectives were clear:

Increase brand engagement both online and offline.

Drive higher foot traffic to Jewel Changi Airport, the airport’s retail and entertainment complex.

Boost retail sales on iShopChangi, Changi Airport’s e-commerce portal.

Insight and Strategy

Understanding the unparalleled dedication of Swifties to Taylor Swift, CAG identified an opportunity to celebrate these fans who often go unnoticed amid the star’s spotlight. The strategy was to elevate the Swifties’ experience by integrating the participative nature of the community with the Changi Airport brand, aligning with the brand’s ethos of providing extraordinary experiences beyond conventional airport services.

Idea: “beJEWELed”

Drawing inspiration from Taylor Swift’s hit song “Bejeweled,” CAG conceived “beJEWELed” as a transformative event where Swifties could upscale their usual karaoke sessions into the biggest sing-along party at Jewel Changi Airport. This marked the first time an airport would metamorphose into an entertainment hub, delivering on CAG’s promise to redefine the airport experience and deliver ’a touch of magic’.

Phases of the Campaign

The Build-up: Inserting the Brand into a Cultural Moment

Leveraging Taylor Swift’s past encounter with Changi Airport where she posted a vlog of her amazement that there was a forest in the airport, CAG initiated a social media campaign inviting Swifties to explore the forest valley in Jewel Changi Airport and share their experiences with the hashtag #SeeTheNewForest.

By tapping into Taylor Swift’s connection with the airport, CAG successfully generated buzz and anticipation for the upcoming event.

As an added incentive for Swifties to visit Changi Airport, CAG ran exclusive retail promotions on iShopChangi and in-store at Changi’s terminals.

Launch: Transforming Jewel into beJEWELed

Building upon the momentum generated, CAG announced “beJEWELed" – an unprecedented sing-along party within Jewel Changi Airport’s forest valley. For just one night, CAG transformed Jewel into a full-on performance venue with sound and lighting fixtures among the flora and fauna.

The event, featuring local artist Joie Tan, local band 53A, and radio DJ Joakim Gomez, celebrated Swifties’ devotion with over two hours of music, trivia, and giveaways, fostering a sense of togetherness and appreciation. In line with the behavior of Swifties, attendees were given a beJEWELed friendship bracelet to exchange with each other. During this magical experience, Swifties were encouraged to continue sending their invitations to Taylor to #SeeTheNewForest.

While Jewel Changi Airport may not be new to travelers and Singapore residents, this was the first time CAG had transformed the space into an exciting entertainment venue. The brand showed versatility and creativity in bringing new experiences to the audience.

Results

With zero sponsorship dollars spent, the campaign exceeded expectations across various metrics:

Brand engagement rate soared by 60%, surpassing industry benchmarks.

Brand reach surged by 190%, amplifying CAG’s visibility.

RSVP targets for "beJEWELed" were surpassed by an astonishing 250%.

Passenger footfall to Changi Airport surged by 40% during the campaign period, eclipsing even the peak holiday season like Chinese New Year.

15% increase in footfall on the day of beJEWELed compared with average Fridays.

The weekend of beJEWELed and Taylor Swift’s concert made it to CAG’s top 5 footfall days in 2024.

iShopChangi’s promotion tailored for Swifties achieved an impressive 98.43% of targets.

Conclusion

CAG delivered the most immersive and exclusive sing-along experience that a Swiftie could experience in the heart of an airport. This not only elevated the airport experience but also cemented its position as a cultural destination.

By celebrating Swifties’ passion for Taylor Swift, CAG demonstrated its commitment to innovation and customer-centricity, setting a new standard for airport marketing. This campaign not only achieved remarkable results but also laid the foundation for future endeavors in engaging and delighting passengers worldwide.
