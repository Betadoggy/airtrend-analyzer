Title: Beyond Bricks And Bytes: Bio Cities Are About To Bloom
Source: Forbes
URL: https://www.forbes.com/sites/timothypapandreou/2024/01/28/beyond-bricks-and-bytes-bio-cities-are-about-to-bloom/
--------------------------------------------------

Our planet’s cities all stand at a crossroads. By 2050, 75% of humanity will call an urban space home. Our current planning, design and building paradigms – resulting in sprawling concrete vertical or horizontal jungles with little if any space for life other than our own – is not only unsustainable, it’s economically unviable and socially inequitable. Most of all it’s inhuman, mechanical and noisy, and it’s anti-nature.

The stark statistics paint a sobering picture. We've spent about 5,000 years building our global urban footprint. We doubled it in the 20th century, and we're poised to double it again by 2050. That’s staggering and sobering. Miles of resource-intensive infrastructure – sand, steel, glass – rise from the dug out holes and ashes of nature, spewing waste and consuming the world’s energy. Our 20th-century resource -intensive urban planning paradigms are obsolete, brittle, and in decline, mirroring the state of our air, water, and surrounding biodiversity. We are the problem, but we are also the solution. This must change, and it must change now.

Infrastructure is our user interface for the planet

We live in an era of unprecedented innovation, we have the data and the tools yet urban planning remains entrenched in linear, cartesian, centralized and outdated thinking. Enter urban AI, transforming how we envision infrastructure through systems thinking. It's not just concrete and steel; it's the very interface through which we experience our urban world through new materials, new processes and new ways of resilient nature based design. We've barely scratched the surface, utilizing generative and predictive AI to understand infrastructure design, material selection, construction and maintenance. Yes, the true frontier ahead lies in integrating urban AI with the biological systems that underpin life itself. Nature is the ultimate infrastructure architect, offering blueprints visible in every cell, cycle and pattern. Let us finally take notice.

Bioplanning: Integrating the Urban Fabric into Nature

Bioplanning transcends mere beautification. It reimagines cities as interconnected cellular superwebs of thriving ecosystems, powered by responsible AI. Humans are co-inhabitants, not conquerors, of this urban organism. Structures, networks, and infrastructure become a living, breathing, transforming system of natural infrastructure. Pollution, emissions, waste outputs cease to exist; inputs are designed to be utilized again and again, mirroring nature's perfect cycles- nothing is wasted, everything is broken down and has a use. This isn't science fiction; it's the essence of bioplanned cities to create healthy, vibrant human experiences.

We’re already seeing intelligent buildings harvesting solar energy, filtering rainwater, and adapting to environmental and climate fluctuations. We have sensors embedded in green facades, monitoring the health of urban forests, while mycelium networks are being utilized to decompose waste organically. Now it’s time to create synergistic systems of symbiosis in the urban form. This interconnected web of smart cities across the globe will be pulsing with real-time data and working towards an urban homeostasis just like in nature. This is not just aesthetically pleasing; it's economically and socially vital. Studies show biophilic design boosts productivity, attracts talent, and lowers maintenance costs. It also increases community interaction of all ages and demographics- a sorely needed fix to our every isolating and polarizing society. Bioplanned cities are magnets for investment and tourism, fostering innovation and economic diversification.

More importantly, they are beacons of equity. Our current cities trap marginalized communities in pockets of pollution and neglect. Bioplanning disrupts this paradigm, prioritizing equitable access to green spaces, clean air, and sustainable infrastructure. Imagine children, playing in tree-lined streets, people strolling and communities making things and growing their own food, and waste managed sustainably – bioplanning isn't a luxury; it's a fundamental human right.

Bio-mobility: Reclaiming the Streets for Life

Bio-mobility is the essential underpinning to bioplanning. A so-called “green” development accessible only by car importing everything from far away is a contradiction and green washing. We need a holistic mobility paradigm that disrupts the very dependence on personal vehicles in urban cores. Streets morph from asphalt arteries into expansive nature filled plazas, pathways snaking through verdant corridors, and families strolling along car-free pathways bathed in sunlight and birdsong.

Bio-mobility reclaims the space between bio-buildings for people, not vehicles. Imagine green-carpeted plazas buzzing with life, open-air markets, and community gardens. AI enabled sensors prioritize a radically different public-private transport system in real-time, seamlessly integrating with various vehicle options right-sized for the trip and purpose. Whether human or AI powered, these vehicles operate at a strolling pace designed to never cut into a pedestrian pathway, these vehicles are guests in this bustling nature-centered urban ecosystem. The cacophony of engines fades, replaced by the rhythmic hum of pedal powered devices, birds and pollinators, children's laughter, and the murmur of a vibrant community.

Bio-mobility is more than technology; it's a cultural shift. It's prioritizing people over vehicles, reclaiming public space for life, and creating cities where every child feels safe playing in the street, every breath is clean, and every step leads to a vibrant haven of human connection. This is the true heart of bioplanning – not just building a city, but building a nature centric life. It’s ironic that people fly thousands of miles to just walk and experience these moments in select spaces across some cities. We know what people love, we just need to make it legal, buildable and scale it with nature at the center.

The next 25 years are pivotal to how we see our emerging city planet unfolds. Let's embrace bioplanning and bio-mobility – the revolution that reclaims our streets, rekindles the human spirit, and rewrites the story of urban mobility, one park, one plaza, one child's laughter at a time. The future is not paved with asphalt; it's an interconnected web of life, pulsating with the rhythm of shared spaces and sustainable mobility. Let's step onto this bioplanned platform and paint a future where every journey is a celebration of life, not a life risking race against the engine. Choose living, choose nature, lets make bio cities our next way of life.
