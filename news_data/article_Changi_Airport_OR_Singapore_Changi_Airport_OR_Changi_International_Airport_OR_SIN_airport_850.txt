Title: Turkish Airlines starts Istanbul-Melbourne flights
Source: Moneycontrol
URL: https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/trends/travel/turkish-airlines-starts-istanbul-melbourne-flights-12411771.html
--------------------------------------------------

Turkish Airlines, the national flag carrier of Türkiye, has started to operate flights to Melbourne, the capital of Victoria State. Melbourne is the airlines’ first destination in the continent of Australia. With this new route, the airline has expanded its operations to six continents, and 346 destinations in 130 countries.

Turkish Airlines will operate three weekly flights to Melbourne, which stands out as the most populated city in Australia, with special introductory prices, via the Singapore Changi International Airport transfer point.

The Australian state of Victoria is home to Australia’s largest Türkiye-born community.

With the flights that will be operated via Singapore transfer point, Turkish Airlines will also hold the distinction of being the sole European airline currently flying to Melbourne Airport.

Turkish Airlines passengers can travel from İstanbul to Melbourne until March 15, 2024, for $1,199 and from Melbourne to İstanbul for $999 by purchasing tickets between until April 15, 2024. The prices applicable under the campaign are the official website fares of Turkish Airlines; however, they may vary at the ticket offices and agencies.

“Today marks a monumental milestone for Turkish Airlines. Australia, with its breathtaking landscapes and warm-hearted people, now becomes the 130th country to join our expansive network, signifying our presence across six continents. Through our unparalleled network, we are thrilled to offer our guests a gateway to global exploration, ensuring that our legacy of exceptional service quality continues to flourish in every corner of the world,” said Prof. Ahmet Bolat, Turkish Airlines board and executive committee chairman.
