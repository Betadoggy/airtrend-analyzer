Title: Khaled arrives in Seoul for five-day working visit
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2024/09/08/khaled-arrives-in-seoul-for-five-day-working-visit
--------------------------------------------------

SEOUL: Defence Minister Datuk Seri Mohamed Khaled Nordin has arrived here on Sunday (Sept 8) to begin his five-day working visit to South Korea.

Upon arrival at Incheon International Airport at approximately 7.45am local time, Khaled and his delegation from the Defence Ministry were welcomed by the Counsellor (Defence) of the Malaysian Embassy in Seoul Col Mohamad Nazri Ahmad.
