NewsletterSearchSearchNewsletterSTYLEPOLITICSCULTUREIDENTITYVIDEOFESTSHOPPINGNewsletterStyleNCT 127's Johnny Suh Wore Trompe-l'oeil Jeans to the Acne Studios SS2025 Show in PFW — See PhotosA statement indeed.By Sara DelgadoSeptember 25, 2024Pierre Suu/Getty ImagesNCT 127's Johnny Suh is continuing his fashion adventures for yet another season. After taking on NYFW with a business casual approach with Thom Browne in 2023 and making his PFW debut last season going shirtless for the Acne Studios show, the Chicago native is back in the City of Lights to support the Swedish brand once more, and he's making sure his ‘fit doesn’t go unnoticed.

Johnny arrived at the venue for the Acne Studios womenswear spring-summer 2025 show, which featured an installation by artist Jonathan Lyndon Chase, wearing trompe-l'œil jeans with multiple printed belts, a fitted tank, a choker with charms, suede brown boots, and a voluminous leather jacket.

Pierre Suu/Getty ImagesOn the beauty front, Johnny opted for smokey brown eyes with defined but fluffed out brows and his black short hair swept back with one strand gracing his forehead.

Pierre Suu/Getty ImagesPrior to the show, Johnny had given fans a peek at what was to come with his airport ‘fit. The star was spotted en route to Paris from South Korea's Incheon Airport on September 23, 2024, wearing Acne Studios' uber-popular Multipocket bag in the large size and white leather.

Han Myung-GuJohnny completed the look with black combat boots, baggy jeans (fitting for an NCT member), and a distressed grey hoodie. He gave photographers a peek at his Tommy Hilfiger underwear while doing the traditional greetings before heading to his gate.

The Chosunilbo JNS/Getty ImagesOf course, Johnny is not the only member of NCT 127 making his way to Europe to attend fashion week. Fellow member Jungwoo also traveled to Italy's fashion capital to attend the Tod's spring-summer 2025 show earlier this week, and newly-blonde Jaehyun has previously represented the group at Prada during Milan Fashion Week. Busy and fashionable bees, indeed.
