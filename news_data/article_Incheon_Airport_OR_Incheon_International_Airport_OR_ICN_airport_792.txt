Title: BTS's V And Park Bo Gum Reunite In Paris And Stun Netizens With Their Rockstar Transformation
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/bts-v-park-bo-gum-reunite-paris-stun-netizens-rockstar-transformation/
--------------------------------------------------

BTS‘s V departed Incheon Airport on June 30 to appear at CELINE‘s spring 2024 menswear show in Paris, France.

As the designer brand’s newest brand ambassador, V arrived at the airport sporting a head-to-toe CELINE outfit, earning praise from fans for his style and manners.

While V was expected to be in appearance at the spring collection show, CELINE’s creative director officially announced that the event was on July 2 canceled amid growing protests in multiple cities throughout France.

While the show was canceled, V was still able to connect with fellow CELINE global ambassador, actor Park Bo Gum, for a quick greeting with VMAN, an offshoot of V Magazine.

Netizens were excited to see the two besties together, who have been able to meet often at CELINE’s events, often along with fellow global ambassador BLACKPINK‘s Lisa, who is currently in London for the group’s headlining performance at 2023 BST Hyde Park.

In V and Park Bo Gum’s most recent appearance together, the always handsome stars, of course, wore CELINE and transformed into runway-ready rockstars in their grungy and chic designer looks.
