Title: Vietnamese Billionaire’s Vietjet Inks $8 Billion Engine Order From CFM
Source: Forbes
URL: https://www.forbes.com/sites/yessarrosendar/2024/10/09/vietnamese-billionaires-vietjet-inks-8-billion-engine-order-from-cfm/
--------------------------------------------------

Vietjet—controlled by Vietnamese billionaire Nguyen Thi Phuong Thao—agreed to purchase about $8 billion worth of aircraft engines from CFM International as the airline steps up expansion plans to cater to the booming travel demand.

The 400 LEAP-1B engines will be installed on airplanes that were ordered in 2016 and 2018, with deliveries expected to start next year, the airline said in a statement.

“Today’s agreement for fuel-efficient engines reinforces our commitment to sustainability,” Thao, chairwoman and founder of Vietjet, said in the statement. The LEAP-1B engine can cut fuel consumption and CO2 emissions by up to 20%, while significantly reducing the airplane’s noise levels, according to Vietjet.

Vietjet is a longtime customer of CFM, a joint venture between France’s Safran Aircraft Engine and U.S. GE Aerospace. It operates a fleet of more than 70 Airbus aircraft powered by CFM56-5B engines.

The airline has been aggressively expanding its fleet to cater to the booming travel demand. In July, the airline also signed a $7.4 billion contract with Airbus to purchase 20 long-range A330neo widebody jets, positioning it to expand into intercontinental routes.

Thao has an estimated net worth of $2.9 billion according to Forbes’ real-time data. In 2011, she launched the airline and drew attention with provocative ads featuring flight attendants in bikinis. The airline then went public in 2017, raising $170 million through an initial public offering on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange.
