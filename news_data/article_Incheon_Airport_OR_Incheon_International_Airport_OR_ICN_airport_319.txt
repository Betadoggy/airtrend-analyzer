Lee Je-hoon shines in silver for JTBC’s The Art of Negotiation

by tccolb

Opening the floor for JTBC’s new office series The Art of Negotiation, we meet our dashing-in-silver Lee Je-hoon (Chief Detective 1958) in the first teaser as he steps in to take charge of the brewing corporate battle.

We thus begin the trailer with Yoon Ju-no’s (Lee Je-hoon) entrance at Incheon airport and we learn through the captions that our famed M&A (mergers and acquisitions) expert is known in the industry as the White Snake. In voiceover, Ju-no explains that, “M&A is as mentioned a war. If war is fought using weapons, M&A is fought using contracts.”

Strategy is key of course in this kind of conflict and the sequence gathers CFO Jang Hyun-sung (Flex x Cop), chairman Sung Dong-il (The Tale of Lady Ok), as well as Ju-no’s partner, attorney Kim Dae-myung (Light Shop) – among other invested parties – to discuss the best tactics for the biggest gain. In the intermittent text screens, we then read: “An invisible war / A meticulous betrayal / Who will seize control of the situation first?”

With casualties being inevitable in combat, our characters carefully deliberate over what can be lost versus what to protect at all costs. We then watch as a plan is quickly hatched by our hero’s opponents to somehow make Ju-no the sacrificial pawn. Thankfully, though, our silver Ju-no isn’t someone who can easily be tarnished and the teaser ends on his confident declaration that his M&A team will prevail.

Production for the drama has PD Ahn Pan-seok (The Midnight Romance in Hagwon, One Spring Night) at the helm with scripts penned by Lee Seung-young. Airing on weekends in just over one month, JTBC’s The Art of Negotiation will be premiering on March 8.
