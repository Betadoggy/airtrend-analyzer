BL drama “Business as Usual” has released a new behind-the-scenes video from its finale!

Based on the web novel of the same name by Moscareto, “Business as Usual” tells the story of two men who once believed they held the weaker position in their relationship. Eight years after their bittersweet romance, they unexpectedly cross paths again at work, still carrying the misunderstandings of their past.

Spoilers

The newly released video starts with Kim Min Joon (Chae Jong Hyeok) running through Incheon International Airport, trying to stop Choi Jin Hwan (Sung Seung Ha) from leaving for London. To film the scene, Chae Jong Hyeok is seen running tirelessly through the terminal, even joking, “I’m filming ‘Running Man’ now.” His commitment to the scene results in a heartfelt and realistic performance that vividly captures the desperation of someone chasing after a loved one.

In the next scene, Min Joon and Jin Hwan reunite, with Jin Hwan ultimately choosing to stay by Min Joon’s side. The video shows the actors collaborating closely with the staff and each other to fine-tune the emotional depth of the characters.

Another scene captures the two actors displaying their natural chemistry off-camera. During breaks, they take photos of each other against the sunny backdrop, highlight their warm friendship behind the scenes.
