Title: Seoul-bound Malaysia Airlines flight forced to return to KLIA
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2024/09/02/seoul-bound-malaysia-airlines-flight-forced-to-return-to-klia
--------------------------------------------------

PETALING JAYA: A Malaysia Airlines(MAS) flight headed for Incheon Airport in Seoul, South Korea was forced to turn back shortly after taking off from Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA)

According to data obtained from flight tracking website FlightRadar24, Malaysia Airlines flight MH66 departed from KLIA just after 12am on Sunday (Sept 2) and returned to the airport at about 1.30am.
