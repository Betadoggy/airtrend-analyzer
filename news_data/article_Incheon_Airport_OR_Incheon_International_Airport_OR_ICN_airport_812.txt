In a recent episode of SBS' "My Little Old Boy," viewers were treated to an exclusive peek into the luxurious world of K-pop sensation GOT7's BAMBAM.

Welcoming Super Junior's HeeChul and entertainer Lee Sang Min into his posh residence located in the upscale Yongsan-gu district of Seoul, the episode unfolded as a captivating journey into the opulence that defines BAMBAM's lifestyle.

Opulence Unveiled: A Luxurious Interior and Pristine Surroundings

As HeeChul and Lee Sang Min crossed the threshold into BAMBAM's abode, their initial reactions were nothing short of astonishment. The interior radiated opulence, boasting a seamless blend of contemporary elegance and artistic finesse.

Every corner of the residence exuded a carefully curated aesthetic, contributing to the overall ambiance of sophistication.

The immaculate condition of the living space further intensified the guests' admiration, setting the stage for a series of revelations about BAMBAM's 'young and rich' lifestyle.

HeeChul's Surprise Gift: A High-End Tequila with a Personal Touch

Seated at the exquisitely adorned dining table, BAMBAM seized the moment to surprise HeeChul with a personalized gift. Presenting a bottle of high-end tequila with HeeChul's name intricately engraved, BAMBAM added a touch of exclusivity to the encounter.

The unexpected gesture not only showcased BAMBAM's generosity but also hinted at the level of thoughtfulness that permeates his lavish lifestyle.

BAMBAM's Timepiece Revelation: A Symbol of Achievement

As the trio engaged in lively conversation, the spotlight turned to BAMBAM's wrist, adorned with a dazzling timepiece. Intrigued by its sparkle, Lee Sang Min couldn't help but inquire about the watch's significance.

BAMBAM, with a warm smile, shared the sentimental story behind the accessory—it was his first-ever purchase, a symbol commemorating GOT7's inaugural victory.

The watch, embellished with diamonds, became a tangible representation of BAMBAM's journey and success in the competitive world of K-pop.

Department Store VVIP: BAMBAM's Shopping Privileges Unveiled

In the midst of banter and camaraderie, the conversation naturally gravitated towards BAMBAM's status as a VVIP at department stores.

Confirming his privileged position, BAMBAM casually disclosed a remarkable perk—he enjoys a 20 percent discount across all brands.
