FacebookX (formerly Twitter)It's happening. Fred again.. is in Australia (again) and is playing a surprise show (again).

The British bleepy-blooper announced atop the Sydney Harbour Bridge on Tuesday morning that he'll be performing within the iconic Sydney Opera House with his right-hand man Tony Friend and possible an orchestra?

It's the Opera House, as if you wouldn't perform with an orchestra.

In the lead up to this huge surprise announcement, Fred Again.. spent a good 48 hours teasing his whereabouts on Instagram, sending fans into an absolute frenzy.

What was in the background of these videos? Which airline was he flying with? Where was he and Henry from Joy Anonymous going? All questions that begged answering.

Thankfully, I am an incredibly whip-smart investigative journalist with a long history of getting into rabbit holes on the internet.

Once Fred again.. started posting his surprise travels, I had an eye on him like a hawk. Post notifications switched on and fast fingers ready to screen grab any key information.

Using my powerful brain I started to work backwards with this information. First up: where was "home" for Henry?

Henry Counsell, one half of Joy Anonymous, is Australian-born and London-based. His home? That's down under, baby. Specifically, Adelaide.

So the QANTAS flight they were on was coming to Australia, but which route were they taking? There was a layover involved, which was at an airport with interesting carpet choices.

The only airport I've experienced in my life was in Singapore, at the (simply ridiculously beautiful) Changi Airport. It's also the kind of airport that I'd imagine would have a pool for travellers to use on layovers.

I crosschecked the photo Fred posted of his friend that went for a dip on the break with photos of the Changi Airport pool online and it matched.

As for the carpet, the pattern was truly interesting and quite unique. Punching "Changi Airport carpet" online led me to a Facebook group that documents the carpets of various airports around the world. Wouldn't you know – a recent post about Changi Airport matched the carpets in Fred again..'s posts.

With the knowledge he was travelling to Australia from London with a stopover in Singapore, it was straight to flight tracking sites.

There's only one QANTAS flight from London to Sydney or Melbourne with that exact stopover, so it's possible he landed in Australia at around 10am on Monday morning. Or maybe Tuesday morning and then did a speedrun through Sydney city to scale the Harbour Bridge in time to post the video announcing the Opera House show.

With the announcement of the show with less than 12 hours before the doors open, the waitlist on the Sydney Opera House website went berserk.

triple j Mornings host Lucy Smith clocked in at around the 25,000 person mark, I checked it again at around 10.30 and saw it had pushed out to 79,000 people. It's possible that it peaked at over 100,000 punters in the queue, if a friend's Instagram story is anything to go by.

The capacity of the Sydney Opera House's concert hall? 2670.

For those of you who managed to snag tickets for the Tuesday night gig, congratulations. You did the near-impossible task of running the Fred Again.. ticket gauntlet and came out victorious.

For those who missed out (or live interstate like me) – surely the crew hasn't travelled halfway across the world for one show.

I'll be keeping my eye on this one, I suggest you do too. I'll let you know as soon as anything happens. Again.
