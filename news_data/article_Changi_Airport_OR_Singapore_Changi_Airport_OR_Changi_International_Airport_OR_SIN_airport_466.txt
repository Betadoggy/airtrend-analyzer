Title: There will be a free Taylor Swift sing-along fan event at Jewel Changi Airport with limited-edition friendship bracelets
Source: CNA
URL: https://cnalifestyle.channelnewsasia.com/entertainment/taylor-swift-fan-event-jewel-changi-airport-384911
--------------------------------------------------

Swifties in Singapore will have the chance to meet each other ahead of Taylor Swift's highly anticipated shows here, thanks to beJEWELed. The fan event, organised by Changi Airport Group (CAG), will be held at Jewel Changi Airport's Shiseido Forest Valley on Mar 1.

Swifties can look forward to singing along to hits such as Love Story, You Belong With Me and Blank Space, and receiving limited-edition friendship bracelets at this event. Hosted by Mediacorp personality Joakim Gomez, beJEWELed will also feature performances by local singer Joie Tan, local band 53A and performing arts studio MADDspace.

Although beJEWELed is a free event, fans will need tickets to participate in it. You can register your interest on this website by 11.59pm on Sunday (Feb 18), in order to receive an email on Feb 19 with the link to get tickets. Tickets will be available on a first come, first served basis.

Fans can also look forward to a slew of deals in the days leading up to and after Taylor Swift's Singapore shows.
