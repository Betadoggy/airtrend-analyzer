RIIZE recently headed to Incheon Airport on August 18, 2023, for a plane to America. The group was scheduled to perform at KCON LA 2023, one of the largest K-Pop festivals globally.

Member Anton quickly gained attention for his adorable visuals. With his youthful looks, he’s just the type that Koreans adore!

He wore a blue beanie with snowflakes on it. A little hot for the sweltering summer Korea is going through currently, but perfect for the group’s Y2K concept.

Fans are already falling in love with his bright smile.

His looks were compared to his SM Entertainment seniors, Girls’ Generation’s YoonA, and SHINee’s Taemin.

With his flawless skin, high nose bridge, and small face, it is no wonder that netizens have made that connection.

His sparkly eyes have also become a feature that sweeps up the fans.

Despite the group’s strong and fierce concept, Anton shows off his duality with his cuteness.

It is no wonder that many Korean netizens are already full of praise about the star. They predict him to be one of the most popular members in the group.
