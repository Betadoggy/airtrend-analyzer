It’s Sunday again, so it must be time for some more Airplane Art.

This week we have a Singapore Airlines Airbus A380 taxiing at London Heathrow as it completes the journey from Singapore Changi Airport.

Singapore Airlines operates a fleet of 12 Airbus A380 in its widebody fleet. The airline also operates the Airbus A350-900, Airbus A350-900URL, Boeing 777-300ER and Boeing 787-10 in its widebody operation, whilst having Boeing 737-800 and Boeing 737-8 MAX in its narrowbody fleet.

Together these aircraft connect Singapore to the world.

This aircraft – 9V-SKN – joined the Singapore Airlines fleet in September 2010, with the aircraft remaining in the fleet since then. It’s been busy, operating out of its home of Singapore Changi Airport to Frankfurt, Sydney and London Heathrow.
