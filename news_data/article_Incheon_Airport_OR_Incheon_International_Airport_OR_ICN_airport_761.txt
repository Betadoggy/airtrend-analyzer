Who doesn’t know the members of K-pop group, Blackpink?

But beyond that, Blinks can also recognise some of their family members instantly.

Like Lisa’s mum, Chitthip Bruschweiler.

Instagram user @zhen_aive bumped into Chitthip recently and shared her experience in a video. Chitthip was spotted queueing at Incheon Airport in a simple stripped outfit and carrying a Louis Vuitton monogram leather bag.

“Excuse me, your daughter is so pretty,” the netizen was heard saying to Chitthip, who politely bowed before replying in Thai: “Thank you.”

Netizens loved how Chitthip responded to the fan at the airport, praising her for being polite in the situation.

“Now I know where Lisa gets her kindness from,” read a comment.

Another also praised Chitthip for her youthful looks and wrote: “[Lisa] got it from her mama.”

Chitthip is regularly seen with Lisa at various Blackpink concerts, like their ongoing Deadline World Tour.

Fans also spotted her at Coachella earlier this year watching Jennie’s performance with Lisa and Rosé, who were seen excitedly chanting their bandmate’s name.

And of course, Chitthip was also there at Lisa's controversial Crazy Horse cabaret show.

Will she be here in Singapore too on Nov 29-30, when Blackpink will be playing the Singapore leg of their world tour?
