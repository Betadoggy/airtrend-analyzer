**[Ministry of Justice – Press Release]

Ministry of Justice Expands Eligible Countries for Automated Immigration Inspection to 18

About 40% of All Foreign Arrivals Can Now Use the System; Registration Locations Increased to Four to Reduce Immigration Queue Times

(For Immediate Release – December 1, 2025)**

The Ministry of Justice (Minister Jeong Sung-ho) announced that, beginning December 1, the number of countries whose nationals may use Korea’s Automated Immigration Inspection System (Smart Entry Service for arrivals) will be significantly expanded from the current four countries (Germany, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau) to a total of 18.

The purpose of this expansion is to reduce long waiting times that foreign visitors to Korea often face during the immigration process.

※ As of 2024, the average waiting time for foreign arrivals at immigration is between 24 min 6 sec and 35 min 38 sec, while peak periods may take up to 92 minutes (Incheon International Airport Corporation).

Newly Added 14 Countries Eligible for Automated Immigration Inspection

The newly added 14 countries are:

United Kingdom, France, Italy, Finland, Portugal, Czech Republic, Netherlands, Hungary, Australia, New Zealand, Mexico, Japan, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

These countries were selected based on multiple factors, including those that already allow Korean nationals to use their automated immigration systems, countries with high levels of people-to-people exchange with Korea, diplomatic relations, and domestic enforcement considerations.

Expansion of Registration Locations

To facilitate pre-registration for automated immigration use, the Ministry has expanded registration locations at Incheon Airport. Previously limited to one location in Terminal 1 Arrivals, registration has now been expanded to Terminal 1 Arrivals (West), Terminal 2 Departures, and Terminal 2 Arrivals (East).

This expansion will improve accessibility, allowing more foreign visitors to conveniently register and use automated immigration inspection upon arrival.

Positive Reactions from Travelers

On the first day of implementation, travelers eligible under the expansion expressed satisfaction:

A Japanese national A said, “Immigration was so simple and fast — it was impressive.”

A Singaporean national B and family said,

“Adults could use the automated gates, and the children used the family-friendly manual counters, so the whole process was efficient and quick.”

An Italian national C said,

“The automated system was fast and convenient, and the clear instructions from registration through usage made everything easier.”

Impact and Future Plans

The Ministry of Justice stated that with this expansion, about 40% of all foreign arrivals will now be eligible to use automated immigration inspection.

It also announced plans to review the pilot operation results at Incheon Airport in January 2026 and decide whether to extend the system to other major airports nationwide.

※ Total number of foreign arrivals in 2024: 16.97 million

Minister Jeong Sung-ho emphasized:

“This expansion of automated immigration inspection is a practical measure to reduce the inconvenience experienced by foreign visitors entering Korea. We will continue to improve processes promptly so that visitors to Korea can enjoy a more convenient and positive immigration experience.”
