The FASTFIVE Magongnaru Branch spans approximately 1,000 pyeong (3,300 sqm) and is located in the Magong Industrial Complex. It boasts a strategic location near Gimpo Airport and Incheon Airport, with direct access to the Magongnaru Station, which serves both Subway Line 9 and the Airport Railroad Express.

The Magongnaru branch is housed in the premium building, Le West CP1. The existing exterior and interior design of the building features a modern and urban aesthetic, which may sometimes feel cold and impersonal. To counteract this, FASTFIVE incorporated natural color tones and curved spatial designs to create a warmer, user-friendly atmosphere that enhances comfort and engagement.

FASTFIVE Magongnaru Branch incorporates the ‘Archway’ concept to maximize spatial flow and openness. By integrating curved structures and arch-shaped design elements throughout the space, it creates a smooth and natural circulation while fostering a warm and inviting atmosphere. This approach transforms the workspace into more than just an office—it becomes an inspiring environment where creative ideas can flourish.

Key Space Design Features

– Main Lounge & Sub-Lounges

A spacious main lounge is complemented by five sub-lounges, strategically placed to enhance workflow efficiency and convenience, providing diverse areas for work and relaxation.

– Meeting Rooms & Seminar Room

A total of 18 shared meeting rooms and a seminar room accommodating up to 40 people are available, supporting meetings and events of various sizes.

– Focus Zones & Phone Booths

Focus zones are provided for individual work, while private phone booths enhance concentration and privacy, boosting work efficiency.

With its harmonized natural design elements and ergonomic spatial planning, FASTFIVE Magongnaru ensures an inviting and efficient workspace that balances professionalism with warmth and comfort for its users.
