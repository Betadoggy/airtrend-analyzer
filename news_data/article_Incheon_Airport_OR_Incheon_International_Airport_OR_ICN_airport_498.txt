Title: ENHYPEN Survive The “Getty Image Curse” During Milan Fashion Week
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/enhypen-survives-getty-image-curse-milan-fashion-week/
--------------------------------------------------

K-Pop idol group ENHYPEN attended Milan Fashion Week for the Prada spring-summer 2025 womenswear show.

The members were announced as Prada’s newest ambassadors in 2023, just three years after debuting. Becoming the face of the luxury brand was a huge milestone for the fourth-generation idol group.

The boys were seen in fall colors at Incheon International Airport, sporting pieces by Prada as they set off for the show.

Photos of the boys at the show garnered much attention online as it seemed like they had survived the “Getty Images Curse,” which is known for its raw and unedited photos, making celebrities look worse than other photos.

Even in individual photos, they were winning!

They even held a mini fan meeting a night before the show to meet with overseas fans!
