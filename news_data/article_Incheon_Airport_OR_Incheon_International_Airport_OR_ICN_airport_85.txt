Title: Blackpink Star Jennie Hits The Top 10 On Multiple Charts With Her New Solo Single
Source: Forbes
URL: https://www.forbes.com/sites/hughmcintyre/2024/10/28/blackpink-star-jennie-hits-the-top-10-on-multiple-charts-with-her-new-solo-single/
--------------------------------------------------

The four members of Blackpink have succeeded on the American charts as a group, and also on their own. The singers have all released material as soloists, and they all claim large fan bases who love to turn their tunes and albums into commercial wins. Jennie, one of the musicians who comprise the outfit, sends her latest tune to several tallies this frame, and it’s a very big, if momentary, smash.

“Mantra,” Jennie’s latest solo single, debuts on at least four Billboard charts this week. The track enters the top 10 on all but one of them—and the cut even manages to become a notable success story for her on that last list as well.

In the U.S., “Mantra” launches highest on the Digital Song Sales ranking. On Billboard’s list of the best-selling tracks in America, Jennie’s latest solo effort opens at No. 10.

Luminate reports that in its first tracking frame, “Mantra” sold 3,100 copies. That proved to be enough to give Jennie her second career top 10 on the Digital Song Sales chart.

The same song was also a global hit as well. In fact, “Mantra” performed better throughout the rest of the world than in the U.S., as Jennie’s fan base is worldwide, and she claims millions of followers in her home nation of South Korea.

“Mantra” starts its time on both of Billboard’s global rankings inside the top 10. Jennie’s latest opens on the Billboard Global 200 at No. 3. She lands one rung higher on the Billboard Global Excl. U.S., where the cut nearly conquered the tally that ranks the most-consumed songs throughout the world, only with all listening data from America removed.

Jennie collects her third top 10 smash on the Billboard Global 200 this week. All but one of her tunes that have landed anywhere on the roster have pushed into the loftiest region.

“Mantra” also reaches the Hot 100 this time around, though just barely. Jennie’s solo single doubles her count of hits on that tally as it opens at No. 98 on the 100-spot list of the most-consumed songs in the country.
