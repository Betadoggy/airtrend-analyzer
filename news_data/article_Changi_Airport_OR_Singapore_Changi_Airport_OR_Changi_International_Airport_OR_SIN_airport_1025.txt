Disney Cruise Line Reveals Largest Ship on Record, and It’s Sold Out for the First Two Quarters

The moment Disney CEO Bob Iger revealed it, jaws dropped: a brand-new cruise ship with enough space for 7,000 guests—nearly double the capacity of its sister ships—set to launch from Singapore as early as next year. Even more surprising? The ship is already sold out for its first two quarters of operation.

In today’s Q3 earnings report, Disney didn’t just announce a new ship. They introduced a game-changing global experience—an entertainment juggernaut on the sea that fans are calling “the floating Disneyland.”

But what makes this announcement so shocking isn’t just the scale of the vessel. It’s the location, timing, and global strategy Disney is signaling—and what it means for the future of Disney Parks & Experiences fans.

This article will break down what you need to know about Disney Adventure: what’s new, how to experience it, why it matters, and the hidden tips that seasoned Disney cruisers are already whispering about.

What’s New: Disney Adventure Sets Sail from Singapore in 2025

Disney Adventure is the name of the newest ship in the Disney Cruise Line fleet—and it’s breaking records before ever leaving port. Announced as part of today’s Q3 earnings call, this massive vessel will launch out of Singapore, marking the company’s first-ever cruise homeport in Southeast Asia.

According to Bob Iger, Disney Adventure is more than just a ship—it’s a floating ambassador for the Disney brand. The ship will welcome up to 7,000 guests per sailing, making it the largest Disney Cruise Line ship to date. (For comparison: Disney Wish, the newest ship in the U.S.-based fleet, only holds around 4,000.)

“This ship allows us to bring the magic of Disney to a brand-new global audience,” Iger said.

Perhaps most notable? Bookings for the ship’s first two quarters are essentially sold out, a signal that international demand for Disney Cruise Line is booming.

How to Experience It: Booking, Tips & Strategy

If you’re dreaming of sailing aboard the Disney Adventure, here’s what you need to know:

Booking Timeline: While the first two quarters are sold out, additional sailings may be announced soon. Stay signed up for email alerts via Disney Cruise Line and follow their regional partners in Asia for updates.

Flight Strategy: Since the ship sails out of Singapore, flying into Changi Airport (voted one of the best airports in the world) offers both convenience and luxury.

Best Time to Visit: The region’s dry season, February through April, offers ideal cruising weather with fewer rain delays.

App Hacks: Download both the Disney Cruise Line Navigator app and the MyDisney Experience app. The former is vital onboard, while the latter may give insight into character meet-and-greets and exclusive cruise content.

Insider Secrets & Local Hacks for the Disney Adventure

Want to cruise like a pro? Here are a few insider tips:

Port-Side Views Are Better: Many seasoned cruisers claim that when sailing out of Singapore, the port side staterooms offer the most beautiful skyline views during departure.

Character Experiences May Differ: Since this ship caters to a new market, you might see regionally popular characters onboard—possibly even exclusive meet-and-greets.

Dining Perks: Expect Pan-Asian cuisine alongside traditional Disney fare. Word is, some chefs from Tokyo Disney Resort are involved in culinary planning.

Shore Excursions Sell Out Fast: Book unique Singapore excursions (like Gardens by the Bay or Sentosa Island tours) as soon as they’re released. These are not your typical Caribbean excursions—experiences are far more culturally immersive and limited in quantity.

This isn’t just about another ship—it’s about Disney planting a flag in Asia. With theme park attendance surging post-pandemic and international travel resuming, this move helps Disney leverage its brand power globally.
