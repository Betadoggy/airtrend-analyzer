Title: Sick of Monday blues? Anand Mahindra shares how you can 'slide right into the week' ahead

Synopsis

Anand Mahindra shares a video of Singapore’s Changi Airport, where passengers can slide to their boarding gates, adding a fun element to travel. Mahindra uses this feature as an analogy to encourage enthusiasm and joy for starting the week positively.

Industrialist Anand Mahindra, renowned for his uplifting social media presence, has once again engaged his followers with an inventive take on combating the notoriously infamous "Monday mornings."

This time, he highlighted a fascinating feature from Singapore’s Changi Airport that allows passengers to take a slide to their boarding gates.

In a post on X, Mahindra shared a video showcasing this playful option at the airport. He used the slide as a metaphor for embracing the week ahead with enthusiasm and joy.

“Apparently, at Singapore’s Changi airport, you can take a slide to your gate. That’s the way to view Monday mornings and a new week. Beat uncertainty by sliding right into it. Singapore's Changi airport allows you to take a slide to your boarding gate,” he wrote.

Live Events

His message resonated strongly with social media users, who flooded the comments section with enthusiastic responses.

One user exclaimed, “This looks so unreal. Amazing,” while another chimed in, “Wow, that's crazy.” The overwhelming consensus echoed admiration for the innovative concept, with many users repeatedly expressing their amazement.

Changi Airport is celebrated for its futuristic amenities. Standing at 12 meters high, this structure is Singapore’s tallest indoor slide, enabling travelers to reach speeds of up to 6 meters per second as they glide between departure and arrival levels.

Made of stainless steel, the slide transforms an otherwise mundane airport experience into a fun adventure, capturing the spirit of travel in a delightful way.
