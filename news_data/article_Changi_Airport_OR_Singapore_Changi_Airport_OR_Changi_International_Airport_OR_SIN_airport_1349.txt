Title: Coldplay’s Latest Music Video Was Filmed In S’pore
Source: 8days.sg
URL: https://www.8days.sg/entertainment/local/coldplay-new-music-video-filmed-singapore-840691
--------------------------------------------------

As if playing six sold-out concerts in Singapore wasn’t tiring enough, British band Coldplay also found the energy to film a music video for their single, ‘Man in The Moon’ here.

A 31-second teaser, released by Visit Singapore, previewed the upcoming music video, which is slated to drop tomorrow (Feb 7).

Running much like a tourism advertisement, the teaser showcases iconic Singapore locations like Jewel Changi Airport, Gardens by the Bay, and of course, our skyline.

Some scenes also showed the band on the floating platform at the Marina Bay Waterfront Promenade.

Now we can't wait to watch the full version.

Fans were thrilled with one commenting, “OMG! In Singapore? Did anyone know?” while another hilariously wrote, “Majulah Singapore!”

Alright Coldplay, we forgive you for not scheduling another round of concerts at the National Stadium this year.
