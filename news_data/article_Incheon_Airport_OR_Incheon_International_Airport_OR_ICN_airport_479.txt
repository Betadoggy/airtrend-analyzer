Title: Adulterous Actress Kim Min Hee Gives Birth To Baby Boy With Hong Sang Soo
Source: Koreaboo.com
URL: https://www.koreaboo.com/news/kim-min-hee-hong-sang-soo-baby/
--------------------------------------------------

Actress Kim Min Hee successfully gave birth to a healthy baby boy on April 8, according to a recent report from Yonhap News. She is now staying at a postpartum care center in Hanam to recover.

Back in January, rumors leaked that the actress was pregnant with director Hong Sang Soo‘s baby. Later, in February, she was spotted at Incheon Airport with director Hong and her baby bump fully visible. It seems like the pregnancy and the birth have gone well, and the couple are now parents to a son.

Adulterous Actress Kim Min Hee’s Extramarital Baby Bump Revealed

The two controversial stars have been in a relationship since 2016. At the time, director Hong was married to a woman — who was the same age as him — and they had a daughter together. Kim Min Hee and Hong Sang Soo admitted to having an affair in 2017. Hong then left his family and has been living with Kim Min Hee for nine years.

The director has filed for a divorce settlement with his ex-wife, but the process was unsuccessful as she effectively refused to accept the necessary documents.

Congratulations to the couple!
