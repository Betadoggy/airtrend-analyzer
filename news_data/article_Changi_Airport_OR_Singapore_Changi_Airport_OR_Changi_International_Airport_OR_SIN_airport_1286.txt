Title: British band Coldplay tease new music video filmed in Singapore
Source: The Straits Times
URL: https://www.straitstimes.com/life/entertainment/british-band-coldplay-tease-new-music-video-filmed-in-singapore
--------------------------------------------------

SINGAPORE - British band Coldplay have dropped a teaser on YouTube on Feb 6 of their latest music video for the song Man In The Moon, and it features scenes shot around Singapore.

The 29-second clip includes locations such as Jewel Changi Airport, People’s Park Complex in Chinatown, Gardens by the Bay, and the rock quartet performing on a stage in the middle of Marina Bay with the Singapore city skyline in the backdrop.

The teaser, which states that the full music video will be released on Feb 7, was also posted on the VisitSingapore YouTube page on Feb 5, with additional remarks that it was “filmed in Singapore”.

On the same day, Los Angeles-based film-maker Ben Mor posted a similar clip on his Instagram page and wrote: “Excited for this one”. He previously directed the music video for Coldplay song Hymn For The Weekend (2015).

Man In The Moon is taken from Moon Music (Full Moon Edition), the bonus edition of their 10th studio album Moon Music (2024).

Coldplay performed six sold-out concerts at the National Stadium in January 2024 as part of their ongoing Music of the Spheres World Tour.

The group will be the latest global music act to release music videos filmed in Singapore.

In 2024, American pop-rock band OneRepublic released a behind-the-scenes video about the inspiration for instrumental track Singapore from their album Artificial Paradise (2024).

In 2021, American pop singer Billie Eilish put out live performance videos that were shot at Gardens by the Bay’s Cloud Forest.
