Title: Jail and fine for two women caught stealing at Changi Airport while in transit to Bali
Source: The Star Online
URL: https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2025/06/16/jail-and-fine-for-two-women-caught-stealing-at-changi-airport-while-in-transit-to-bali
--------------------------------------------------

SINGAPORE (The Straits Times/ANN): Two travellers flew into Singapore from Kolkata, India, on June 2 and stayed in transit at Changi Airport before continuing their journey to Bali.

While in transit, the pair stole from shops at the airport but were quickly caught by the authorities.
