Title: Queen Of Tears actress Kim Ji-won greeted by crowd of fans at Changi Airport
Source: CNA
URL: https://cnalifestyle.channelnewsasia.com/entertainment/kim-jiwon-singapore-bulgari-event-390096
--------------------------------------------------

Queen Of Tears star Kim Ji-won was greeted by large crowds as she arrived at Singapore Changi Airport on Sunday (May 12) night with eager fans recording the event on their phones.

The actress played the main character, Hong Hae-in, in the popular series, starring opposite Kim Soo-hyun, who played her husband. The series became the highest rated tvN drama of all time following its finale on Apr 28.

She arrived at Terminal 4, wearing a mostly all-white outfit, with a blue striped overshirt and cap. She was also seen smiling and waving at the fans there.

Hours earlier, the actress was also greeted by a large crowd of fans at Seoul's Incheon Airport where she boarded her flight for Singapore.

Kim is said to be in Singapore for a perfume launch event by luxury brand Bulgari on Monday. It is unclear how long she will be in town.
