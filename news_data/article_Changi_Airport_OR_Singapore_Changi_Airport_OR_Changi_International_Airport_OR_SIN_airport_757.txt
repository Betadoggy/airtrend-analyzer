Title: Singapore's Changi Airport to invest $2.3 bn to improve services
Source: The Times of India
URL: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/business/singapores-changi-airport-to-invest-2-3-bn-to-improve-services/articleshow/115052730.cms
--------------------------------------------------

Synopsis

Changi Airport in Singapore has announced a S$3 billion investment plan to enhance its services over the next six years. The upgrades will focus on areas like baggage handling and check-in procedures at Terminals 1 through 4. To fund these improvements, the airport will gradually increase charges, including passenger fees, starting in 2025.
